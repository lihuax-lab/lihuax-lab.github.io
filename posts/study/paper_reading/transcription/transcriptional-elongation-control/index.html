SUMMARY｜摘要#

Original: The elongation stage of transcription by RNA polymerase II (RNA Pol II) is central to the regulation of gene expression in response to developmental and environmental cues in metazoan. Dysregulated transcriptional elongation has been associated with developmental defects as well as disease and aging processes. Decades of genetic and biochemical studies have painstakingly identified and characterized an ensemble of factors that regulate RNA Pol II elongation. This review summarizes recent findings taking advantage of genetic engineering techniques that probe functions of elongation factors in vivo. We propose a revised model of elongation control in this accelerating field by reconciling contradictory results from the earlier biochemical evidence and the recent in vivo studies. We discuss how elongation factors regulate promoter-proximal RNA Pol II pause release, transcriptional elongation rate and processivity, RNA Pol II stability and RNA processing, and how perturbation of these processes is associated with developmental disorders, neurodegenerative disease, cancer, and aging.

中文: RNA Pol II（RNA Pol II）的转录延伸阶段，是后生动物响应发育和环境信号、调控基因表达的核心环节。转录延伸失调与发育缺陷、疾病及衰老过程有关。数十年来，遗传学和生物化学研究逐步鉴定并解析了一系列调控 RNA Pol II 延伸的因子。本综述总结了近期利用遗传工程技术在体内探究延伸因子功能的研究。通过梳理早期生化证据与近期体内研究之间看似矛盾的结果，我们在这一快速发展的领域提出了修订后的延伸调控模型。我们讨论延伸因子如何调控启动子近端 RNA Pol II 的暂停释放、转录延伸速率和过程性、RNA Pol II 稳定性及 RNA 加工，以及这些过程受到扰动时与发育障碍、神经退行性疾病、癌症和衰老的关联。

INTRODUCTION｜引言#

Original: The first link between transcriptional elongation control and human cancer was identified in 1996, when the eleven-nineteen lysine-rich leukemia (ELL) gene, a translocation partner of the mixed lineage leukemia (MLL) gene, was found to encode a factor controlling elongation stage of transcription by RNA polymerase II (RNA Pol II).1 This initial report suggested that the elongation stage of transcription is a key regulatory step in gene expression and plays a central role in development, and its dysregulation can cause disease pathogenesis, which had not previously been appreciated.1 Since, it has been demonstrated that the diverse MLL translocation partners found in pediatric leukemias include many subunits of the ELL-containing super elongation complex (SEC), which regulates the release of paused RNA Pol II into productive transcriptional elongation via the activity of the RNA Pol II C-terminal domain (CTD) kinase cyclin-dependent kinase 9 (CDK9) within SEC.2,3 During the past two decades since linking the transcription elongation factor ELL translocations to leukemic pathogenesis, other studies have demonstrated the significance of transcriptional elongation control in the regulation of gene expression and identified various elongation factors regulating the elongation stage of transcription within physiological contexts relevant to human health and disease. In this review, we provide an up-to-date and comprehensive picture of the mechanisms by which RNA Pol II elongation factors regulate gene expression, as well as their functional roles in development, disease, and as recently been demonstrated in the aging process. Collectively, these findings further affirm the initial observation that the elongation stage of transcription is a key regulatory step in gene expression and its perturbation drive disease pathogenesis.1

中文: 1996 年，研究者首次建立了转录延伸调控与人类癌症之间的联系：混合谱系白血病（MLL）基因的易位伙伴——十一-十九富赖氨酸白血病（ELL）基因，被发现编码一种调控 RNA Pol II（RNA Pol II）转录延伸阶段的因子。1 这项最初的研究提示，转录延伸是基因表达的关键调控步骤，在发育中发挥核心作用，其失调可导致疾病发生；这一点此前尚未受到重视。1 此后研究表明，儿童白血病中发现的多种 MLL 易位伙伴，实际上包括含 ELL 的超级延伸复合物（SEC）的许多亚基。SEC 通过其中的 RNA Pol II C 端结构域（CTD）激酶——细胞周期蛋白依赖性激酶 9（CDK9）的活性，调控暂停的 RNA Pol II 释放并进入有效转录延伸。2,3 自 ELL 易位与白血病发病机制建立联系以来的二十余年中，其他研究进一步证实了转录延伸控制对基因表达调控的重要性，并鉴定出多种在与人类健康和疾病相关的生理情境中调控转录延伸的因子。本综述全面介绍 RNA Pol II 延伸因子调控基因表达的最新机制，以及它们在发育、疾病和近期揭示的衰老过程中的功能。这些发现共同支持最初的观点：转录延伸是基因表达的关键调控步骤，其扰动能够驱动疾病发生。1

Original: The transcription cycle catalyzed by RNA Pol II is a remarkably conserved mechanism for gene expression within eukaryotes. This cycle begins with the initiation of transcription, followed by promoter-proximal pausing; pause release allows productive elongation and concurrent transcript processing, and the cycle ends with transcription termination. For control of transcription, both the initiation and elongation steps have been shown to be the key regulatory steps. During the elongation step, RNA Pol II traverses genetic distances up to several hundreds of kilobases, and its speed and processivity throughout the genome must be exact for accurate and timely synthesis of a given transcript.

中文: RNA Pol II 催化的转录周期是真核生物中高度保守的基因表达机制。该周期始于转录起始，随后进入启动子近端暂停；暂停释放后，RNA Pol II 开展有效延伸，同时进行转录本加工，最后以转录终止结束。转录起始和延伸均已被证实是关键的调控步骤。在延伸阶段，RNA Pol II 可沿 DNA 行进数百 kb；其在全基因组中的速度和过程性必须受到精确控制，才能准确、及时地合成相应转录本。

Original: RNA Pol II catalyzes the processive addition of nucleotides to nascent transcripts as it traverses DNA wrapped around histone octamers. Meanwhile, these transcripts also undergo post-transcriptional processing, including 5′ capping and splicing. In addition to these elongation events, RNA Pol II is known to pause at promoter-proximal regions around the first nucleosomes in most metazoans, including human, mouse, and fly.3–5 Paused RNA Pol II is then either released into productive elongation at gene bodies or removed from promoter-proximal regions without production of full-length transcripts.4 This promoterproximal pause/release of RNA Pol II is a fundamental regulatory step for precision both in timing of gene expression and in modulation of expression levels. It is therefore important to understand how elongating RNA Pol II achieves processivity, nucleosomal passage, post-transcriptional processing, and promoter-proximal pause/release to generate properly processed transcript at the right time.

中文: RNA Pol II 沿缠绕于组蛋白八聚体上的 DNA 行进时，催化核苷酸持续添加至新生转录本。与此同时，这些转录本还经历加工，包括 5′ 端加帽和剪接。除这些延伸事件外，在人、小鼠和果蝇等大多数后生动物中，RNA Pol II 还会在第一个核小体附近的启动子近端区域暂停。3–5 随后，暂停的 RNA Pol II 要么释放并在基因体内进行有效延伸，要么在未生成全长转录本的情况下从启动子近端区域移除。4 RNA Pol II 的这种启动子近端暂停/释放，是精确控制基因表达时机和表达水平的基本调控步骤。因此，有必要理解延伸中的 RNA Pol II 如何维持过程性、穿越核小体、协调转录本加工及启动子近端暂停/释放，从而在恰当的时间产生经过正确加工的转录本。

Original: Over the past several decades, a variety of elongation factors have been identified using in vitro transcription assays, genetics, and proteomics approaches.3–5 As we will discuss in this review, these elongation factors interact with the transcribing RNA Pol II to control its elongation activity, processivity, and transcript maturation properties. Notably, the investigation of elongation factors in vivo had been challenging for decades because many of these factors are essential for cellular growth, and their deletion or reduction by shRNA (short hairpin RNA) results in the loss of viability. However, due to the recent advent of targeted protein degradation approaches,6,7 our mechanistic understanding of elongation inside the cells, rather than in the reconstituted system lacking many of the essential factors, has been dramatically advanced. The success of these approaches can be attributed to the rapidity with which transcription factors are depleted, within 30 min to a few hours. This rapid depletion allows for the observation of primary phenotypes decoupled from any secondary or indirect effects caused by growth defects upon long-term depletion. Also, when a targeted gene is genetically tagged with a degron peptide, off-target effects can be significantly minimized, allowing for identification of the true, direct target of a given factor. While some of the results obtained using these degradation approaches are consistent with previous findings, there have also been many contradictory observations between reconstituted system in test tubes and what actually happens inside the cells. In this review, we aim to reconcile these contradictions between in vitro and in vivo studies, providing a new model for the molecular mechanisms of elongation factor function in living cells. In particular, we focus on the elongation factors such as CDK9 and bromodomain and extraterminal (BET) proteins, SEC, CDK12/13 kinases, negative elongation factor (NELF), suppressor of Ty 5 (SPT5), SPT4, SPT6, RNA-Pol-II-associated factor 1 (PAF1) complex (PAF1C), and Integrator (Figure 1). We also discuss how misregulation of elongation is associated with development, aging, and disease pathogenesis.

中文: 过去数十年间，研究者利用体外转录实验、遗传学和蛋白质组学方法鉴定出多种延伸因子。3–5 如本综述所述，这些因子与正在转录的 RNA Pol II 相互作用，调控其延伸活性、过程性及转录本成熟。长期以来，在体内研究延伸因子一直具有挑战性：许多因子对细胞生长必不可少，删除相应基因或用短发夹 RNA（shRNA）降低其表达，会导致细胞丧失活力。近年来，靶向蛋白降解方法的出现，6,7 极大推进了我们对细胞内转录延伸机制的认识，使研究不再局限于缺少许多必需因子的体外重构体系。这些方法的优势在于，能在 30 min 至数小时内快速耗竭转录因子，从而观察其直接引起的初级表型，减少长期耗竭造成生长缺陷后出现的次级或间接效应。此外，通过遗传工程在靶基因编码产物上添加降解标签，可显著减少脱靶效应，有助于鉴定给定因子真正的直接作用靶标。采用这些降解方法获得的部分结果与以往发现一致，但试管中的重构体系与细胞内实际情况之间，也出现了许多相互矛盾的观察。本综述旨在协调这些体外和体内研究结果，为活细胞中延伸因子的分子作用机制提出新的模型。我们重点讨论 CDK9、含溴结构域和额外末端结构域的 BET 蛋白、SEC、CDK12/13 激酶、负性延伸因子（NELF）、Ty 抑制因子 5（SPT5）、SPT4、SPT6、RNA Pol II 相关因子 1（PAF1）复合物（PAF1C）以及 Integrator（图 1），并讨论延伸调控异常与发育、衰老和疾病发生的关系。

CDK9-CONTAINING POSITIVE ELONGATION FACTORS｜含有 CDK9 的正性转录延伸因子#

Original: CDK9 and cyclin T form a complex called positive transcription elongation factor b (P-TEFb) in eukaryotic cells, function of which has been shown to be central to stimulate RNA Pol II elongation genome-wide. Unlike cell-cycle-related cyclins, whose oscillating protein levels regulate the activity of partner kinases, the protein levels of transcription-related cyclins, including cyclin ‘‘T’’ (transcriptional), do not oscillate in growing cells. Instead, the CDK9 kinase is regulated via more complex mechanisms that involve the interaction with coactivators, including BRD4 (bromodomain-containing protein 4) and the SEC family.3 Because CDK9 plays key roles in disease processes, researchers have proposed therapeutic approaches that make use of inhibitors developed to target CDK9 or its coactivators BRD4 and SEC family. In this section, we discuss our current understanding of how CDK9 kinase activity is regulated by BRD4 and SEC. Functions of the other two transcriptional elongation kinases CDK12 and CDK13 are also discussed. For other aspects of CDK9 regulation including the inactive 7SK snRNP (small nuclear ribonucleoprotein) complex, please refer to the recent review.8

中文: 在真核细胞中，CDK9 与细胞周期蛋白 T（细胞周期蛋白 T）形成正性转录延伸因子 b（P-TEFb）复合物，后者在促进全基因组范围的 RNA Pol II 延伸中发挥核心作用。细胞周期相关的细胞周期蛋白通过蛋白水平的周期性变化调控配对激酶的活性；而与转录相关的细胞周期蛋白，包括名称中“T”代表转录的 细胞周期蛋白 T，在生长细胞中的蛋白水平并不发生这种周期性变化。CDK9 激酶受到更复杂的机制调控，其中包括与 BRD4（含溴结构域蛋白 4）和 SEC 家族等共激活因子的相互作用。3 由于 CDK9 在疾病过程中发挥关键作用，研究者提出了利用 CDK9 或其共激活因子 BRD4、SEC 家族抑制剂的治疗策略。本节介绍目前对 BRD4 和 SEC 调控 CDK9 激酶活性的认识，也讨论另外两种转录延伸激酶 CDK12 和 CDK13 的功能。关于 CDK9 调控的其他方面，包括无活性的 7SK 小核核糖核蛋白（snRNP）复合物，请参阅近期综述。8

Identification of CDK9｜CDK9 的鉴定#

Original: Several early studies of transcription identified the CDK9-cyclin T complex P-TEFb as a positive regulator of transcriptional elongation.Using in vitro transcription assays,P-TEFb was identified from Drosophila nuclear extract as a target of the pan-CDK inhibitor DRB (5,6-dichloro-1-b-D-ribofuranosylbenzimidazole), an adenosine analog that blocks RNA Pol II elongation.9–11 The CDK9-cyclin T complex was also identified asthe host cell binding partner ofthe human immunodeficiency virus type 1 (HIV-1) protein Tat, which stimulates RNA Pol II elongation at the viral promoter long terminal repeat (LTR).11–14 The BUR1 (bypass upstream activating sequence requirement 1) kinase and BUR2 cyclin, homologs of CDK9 and cyclin T in S. cerevisiae, were identified through a yeast genetic screen that isolated transcriptional regulators.15 While an early study first demonstrated that CDK9 phosphorylates the RNA Pol II CTD,16 a variety of other CDK9 phosphorylation targets have been identified through phosphoproteomics both in vivo and in vitro.17–20 CDK9 kinase activity is considered to be essential for RNA Pol II release, as acute inhibition of CDK9 kinase activity blocks the genome-wide release of promoter-proximal RNA Pol II in vivo.21–23

中文: 早期的多项转录研究将 CDK9–细胞周期蛋白 T 复合物 P-TEFb 鉴定为转录延伸的正性调控因子。研究者利用体外转录实验，在果蝇核提取物中发现 P-TEFb 是广谱 CDK 抑制剂 DRB（5,6-二氯-1-β-D-呋喃核糖基苯并咪唑）的作用靶标；DRB 是一种可阻断 RNA Pol II 延伸的腺苷类似物。9–11 CDK9–细胞周期蛋白 T 复合物还被鉴定为人类免疫缺陷病毒 1 型（HIV-1）Tat 蛋白的宿主细胞结合伙伴。Tat 可促进病毒长末端重复序列（LTR）启动子处的 RNA Pol II 延伸。11–14 在酿酒酵母中，CDK9 和细胞周期蛋白 T 的同源物 BUR1（bypass upstream activating sequence requirement 1）激酶和 BUR2 细胞周期蛋白，是通过筛选转录调控因子的酵母遗传筛选鉴定的。15 早期研究首先证明 CDK9 可磷酸化 RNA Pol II CTD，16 随后的体内和体外磷酸化蛋白质组学研究又鉴定出多种其他底物。17–20 CDK9 激酶活性被认为是 RNA Pol II 释放所必需的，因为在体内急性抑制该活性，会在全基因组范围阻断启动子近端 RNA Pol II 的释放。21–23

CDK9 active complex 1: BRD4-CDK9｜CDK9 活性复合物 1：BRD4-CDK9#

Original: The BET domain protein family consists of BRD2, BRD3, BRD4, and the testis-specific BRDT (bromodomain testis associated) in mammalian cells. These proteins share two structurally conserved tandem bromodomains at their N termini that recognize acetylated lysine residues within histone tails.24 Interaction between BRD4 and the CDK9-cyclin T complex was initially identified through purification of BRD4 from nuclear extracts,25 and this interaction was subsequently found to be required for the stimulation of transcriptional elongation in vitro.26 Biochemical studies demonstrated that the CDK9-cyclin T complex directly interacts with the conserved C-terminal motifs of BRD4 and BRDT, called the P-TEFb-interacting domain (PID),27 and further demonstrated that the PID alone is sufficient to stimulate CDK9 kinase activity in vitro.28 These results suggest a model in which BRD4’s C-terminal PID enhances CDK9 kinase activity, whereas BRD4’s N-terminal bromodomains interact with histone acetylation-enriched chromatin regions, including active promoters and enhancers. BRD4 contains a large intrinsically disordered region between bromodomains and PID.29 The formation of nuclear condensates involving this unstructured region and its function in cancers have been reviewed elsewhere.30

中文: 在哺乳动物细胞中，BET 蛋白家族包括 BRD2、BRD3、BRD4 和睾丸特异性 BRDT（睾丸相关溴结构域蛋白）。这些蛋白的 N 端均含有两个结构保守的串联溴结构域，可识别组蛋白尾部的乙酰化赖氨酸残基。24 研究者最初通过从核提取物中纯化 BRD4，发现其与 CDK9–细胞周期蛋白 T 复合物相互作用；25 随后证实，这种相互作用是体外促进转录延伸所必需的。26 生化研究表明，CDK9–细胞周期蛋白 T 复合物直接结合 BRD4 和 BRDT 的保守 C 端基序，即 P-TEFb 相互作用结构域（PID），27 而单独的 PID 就足以在体外增强 CDK9 激酶活性。28 这些结果支持如下模型：BRD4 的 C 端 PID 增强 CDK9 激酶活性，其 N 端溴结构域则与活跃启动子、增强子等富含乙酰化组蛋白的染色质区域结合。BRD4 的溴结构域与 PID 之间还有一段较大的内在无序区。29 该无序区参与核内凝聚体形成及其在癌症中的作用，已在其他综述中讨论。30

Original: The function of BRD4 bromodomains in transcriptional regulation has been extensively studied for more than a decade now, after two BET bromodomain inhibitor lead compounds (JQ1 and I-BET151) were shown to have anti-cancer activity.31,32 A knockdown screen targeting chromatin regulators also independently showed that BRD4 depletion results in growth suppression in aggressive leukemia.33 Despite widespread chromatin localization of BET proteins, BET bromodomain inhibition is effective for downregulation of a small subset of genes, including the oncogene MYC and its target genes.32–34 This finding, in combination with the BRD4-Mediator interaction,25,35 led to the identification of super-enhancers, genomic elements that are co-occupied by exceptionally high levels of BRD4 and the Mediator complex.36 BET bromodomain inhibition results in preferential loss of BRD4 at super-enhancers, which contributes to the selective downregulation of the MYC gene.36 Consistent with this result, JQ1 resistance observed in certain cancer cell lines is associated with an enhanced interaction between BRD4 and Mediator that prevents the loss of BRD4 from super-enhancers otherwise induced by JQ1.37

中文: 自两种 BET 溴结构域抑制剂先导化合物 JQ1 和 I-BET151 被证实具有抗癌活性以来，研究者已对 BRD4 溴结构域的转录调控功能开展了十余年的广泛研究。31,32 另一项针对染色质调控因子的敲低筛选也独立发现，耗竭 BRD4 可抑制侵袭性白血病的生长。33 尽管 BET 蛋白广泛定位于染色质，抑制其溴结构域却主要有效下调少数基因，包括癌基因 MYC 及其靶基因。32–34 结合 BRD4 与 Mediator 的相互作用，25,35 这一发现促成了超级增强子的鉴定：这些基因组元件被极高水平的 BRD4 和 Mediator 复合物共同占据。36 抑制 BET 溴结构域会优先使 BRD4 从超级增强子上丢失，进而有助于选择性下调 MYC。36 与此一致，某些癌细胞系的 JQ1 耐药性与 BRD4–Mediator 相互作用增强有关，这种增强的相互作用阻止了原本由 JQ1 诱导的 BRD4 从超级增强子上脱离。37

Original: Early studies suggested that the inhibition of BET bromodomain by JQ1 results in increased RNA Pol II pausing at a small subset of genes.36,38 In contrast, the degradation of BET proteins by dBET6 results in genome-wide induction of pausing.39 This BET degradation phenocopies CDK9 inhibition,39,40 suggesting that CDK9 regulates RNA Pol II pause/release through BET proteins. Direct and specific rapid degradation of BRD4 using either AID (auxin-inducible degron) or dTAG (degradation tag) strategies confirmed that the pausing phenotype induced by BET degradation is specifically due to loss of BRD4,41–43 but not BRD2 or BRD3, which lack interaction with CDK9.42 A recent genetic complementation study demonstrated that RNA Pol II pausing induced by BRD4 depletion can be rescued by bromodomain-less, C-terminal fragment of BRD4.44 This fragment can interact with the CDK9-cyclin T complex but not acetylated histones.44 Thus, BRD4-CDK9 promotes RNA Pol II release independently of chromatin association via binding to acetylated histones. This ‘‘acetyl-unbound’’ layer contrasts the previously described function of ‘‘acetyl-bound’’ BRD4 at super enhancer-controlled genes.36 The acetyl-bound and acetyl-un- bound BRD4 are likely to have distinct mechanisms to regulate RNA Pol II elongation. Original: How NELF plays a positive role in elongation is an important open question. The NELF-E tentacle is also involved in condensate formation at stress conditions.97 This suggests that partitioning of NELF may alter the interaction with CBC and nascent transcripts. Original: In addition to 5′ end capping regulation, NELF has also been shown to regulate 3′ end processing. Roles for SPT5 in the regulation of promoter-proximal pausing and processive elongation｜SPT5 在启动子近端暂停和过程性延伸调控中的作用#

Original: More than 25 years ago, the elongation factor SPT5 was initially identified and characterized in two independent studies. A newly recognized, conserved role for SPT5 in RNA Pol II stabilization｜SPT5 稳定 RNA Pol II 的新发现保守功能#

Original: Rapid depletion strategies have delivered a breakthrough in our understanding of how SPT5 functions in mammalian cells. Recent work revealed that SPT5 loss results in degradation of the largest RNA Pol II subunit B1 (RPB1),108,116 for which the catalytic activity of the unfoldase VCP (valosin containing protein)/p97 is required.108 Because SPT5 functions to stabilize RNA Pol II, it was crucial to determine whether transcriptional defects observed in SPT5-depleted cells result from RNA Pol II degradation directly or from degradation-independent changes in RNA Pol II function. Blocking RNA Pol II degradation with the VCP inhibitor NMS-873 revealed a significant reduction in the elongation rate upon SPT5 depletion, decoupled from the effects of RNA Pol II destabilization.108 This result suggests that in addition to stabilizing RNA Pol II, SPT5 also independently functions to stimulate its elongation rate in vivo. We briefly summarize recent findings regarding the relationship between transcriptional elongation and aging and provide some future perspective for studies looking at the aging process and the role of the elongation factors described above.

(B) Changes in RNA Pol II elongation rates and the abundance of long transcripts over lifetime are indicated. Slowing down the RNA Pol II elongation rate results in extended lifespan.

A role for RNA Pol II elongation rate in the mechanism of aging｜RNA Pol II 延伸速率在衰老机制中的作用#

Original: A recent study provided a catalog of RNA Pol II elongation rates in both young and old model organisms.290 High-throughput rate measurements were achieved simply using total RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) data,290 based on the previous finding that intronic RNA-seq signal exhibits a saw-tooth pattern, the slope of which can be used to compute relative changes in RNA Pol II elongation rate (Figure 6A).21,291 This study demonstrated that rates of RNA Pol II elongation increase upon aging in human, rat, mouse, fly, and worm.290 Notably, flies and worms with ‘‘slow RNA Pol II’’ due to mutations in a RNA Pol II subunit exhibited an extended lifespan.290 This genetic evidence strongly suggests that an elevated RNA Pol II elongation rate is not only a conserved feature of aging but also part of a conserved aging mechanism. It also suggests that lifespan could be extended by slowing down RNA Pol II in other species (Figure 6B). It remains unclear how a ‘‘fast RNA Pol II’’ could cause or worsen the effects of aging, but increasing RNA Pol II speed over lifespan is attributed in part to a partial loss of histone. Deprivation of nucleosome occupancy has been observed in aged organisms from yeast to human.292,293 In yeast, histone loss upon aging resulted in gene upregulation294 and overexpression of histone proteins extended lifespan.292 Consistent with these precedent studies, overexpression of histone H3 was shown to reduce RNA Pol II elongation rates and suppress senescence in human cells and to extend lifespan in flies.290 These results suggest an aging model in which (1) a decreasing supply of histone proteins causes partial loss of nucleosome occupancy, (2) rates of RNA Pol II elongation are increased as a result of loss of nucleosomal obstacles to transcription in gene bodies, and (3) increased RNA Pol II speed causes or worsens the effects of aging through unknown mechanisms. Given that RNA Pol II elongation rate is regulated by various elongation factors including SPT5, PAF1C, SPT6, and SEC, development and use of potent inhibitors targeting elongation factors will be a new direction of future studies aiming to address the challenge of slowing down aging processes. Increasing histone gene expression may also be an alternative way to slow down RNA Pol II to ‘‘slow down aging.’’

中文: 近期一项研究系统整理了年轻和老年模式生物中的 RNA Pol II 延伸速率。290 该研究仅利用总 RNA 测序（RNA-seq）数据便实现了高通量速率测量；290 其依据是此前的发现：内含子 RNA-seq 信号呈锯齿状分布，可根据斜率计算 RNA Pol II 延伸速率的相对变化（图 6A）。21,291 研究发现，在人、大鼠、小鼠、果蝇和线虫中，RNA Pol II 延伸速率均随衰老升高。290 尤其值得关注的是，因 RNA Pol II 亚基突变而具有“慢速 RNA Pol II”的果蝇和线虫，表现出寿命延长。290 这一遗传证据强烈提示，RNA Pol II 延伸加速不仅是衰老的保守特征，也是保守衰老机制的一部分；它还提示，减慢其他物种的 RNA Pol II 可能延长其寿命（图 6B）。目前仍不清楚“快速 RNA Pol II”如何引发或加剧衰老，但 RNA Pol II 速度随年龄升高，部分可归因于组蛋白丢失。从酵母到人类，衰老生物中均观察到核小体占据减少。292,293 酵母衰老时，组蛋白丢失引起基因表达上调，294 而过表达组蛋白可延长寿命。292 与这些早期研究一致，过表达组蛋白 H3 可降低人细胞中的 RNA Pol II 延伸速率并抑制细胞衰老，也可延长果蝇寿命。290 这些结果支持一种衰老模型：（1）组蛋白供应减少，导致部分核小体占据丢失；（2）基因体内核小体对转录的阻碍减少，使 RNA Pol II 延伸速率升高；（3）RNA Pol II 加速通过尚不清楚的机制引起或加剧衰老。由于 SPT5、PAF1C、SPT6 和 SEC 等多种延伸因子调控 RNA Pol II 的延伸速率，开发并应用针对这些因子的强效抑制剂，将是探索如何减缓衰老的新方向。提高组蛋白基因的表达，也可能是使 RNA Pol II 减速、进而“减缓衰老”的另一条途径。

Loss of long transcripts as you age｜随衰老出现的长转录本减少#

Original: On the other hand, a strong correlation between aging and lower abundance of ‘‘long transcripts’’ has recently been demonstrated (Figure 6B).295–297 In an accelerated aging model, mice that possess mutations in the nucleotide excision DNA repair pathway exhibit a preferential loss of mRNA expression from long genes in liver tissues, while dietary restrictions that increase their lifespan rescue this length-associated downregulation.296 Consistently, the aged mouse liver was shown to exhibit an approximately 1.5-fold reduction in total RNA synthesis.297 Productive elongation declined and Ser2P-associated RNA Pol II occupancy also increased in a gene-length-dependent manner in the aged mouse liver,297 suggesting that RNA Pol II becomes stalled in long genes upon aging. The mechanism of this lengthassociated RNA Pol II stalling can be explained by the stochasticity of DNA damage events in the genome: long genes statistically accumulate more DNA lesions, which pose obstacles to productive transcription, than short genes purely as a function of their greater length. Consistent with this model, the study showed that UV-induced DNA damage caused transcriptional stalling in long genes, and that the normally aged liver exhibited an activation of DNA repair pathway.297 These results strongly suggest that aging is associated with RNA Pol II stalling in long genes, but it is currently unknown whether this association is causal, and if so whether RNA Pol II stalling in long genes is a cause or a consequence of aging. Therapeutic approaches to aging can vary depending on these conclusions. Although RNA Pol II elongation speed increases upon aging, it is unclear whether RNA Pol II stalling in long genes and a fast RNA Pol II can co-exist in aged animal. As one possible complication, slowing down RNA Pol II elongation rates to extend lifespan might affect the efficiency of the DNA damage repair mechanisms that are required for productive transcription in long genes, with increasing requirement as lifespan extends.

Original: The loss of long transcripts which is a hallmark of aged cells is reminiscent of the processivity defects observed in PAF1C, SPT6, or CDK12-deficient cells (please see the SPT6 section).

中文: 长转录本减少是衰老细胞的一个特征，这与 PAF1C、SPT6 或 CDK12 缺失细胞中观察到的过程性缺陷相似（参见 SPT6 一节）。

CONCLUDING REMARKS｜结语#

Original: We published in 1996 the first link between transcriptional elongation control and human cancer,1 demonstrating that the elongation stage of transcription is a key regulatory step in development and its dysregulation can drive disease pathogenesis. Since then, we have demonstrated that diverse translocation within the MLL gene causing leukemia is into many subunits of the SEC,regulating transcriptional elongation control and pause release and its misregulation causing cancer.2,3 Collectively,our studies and that of colleagues in the field as described in this review have demonstrated the importance of transcription elongation control not only in developmental regulation of gene expression, but also during disease pathogenesis and in aging.

中文: 我们于 1996 年首次报道了转录延伸控制与人类癌症之间的联系，1 表明转录延伸是发育过程中的关键调控步骤，其失调能够驱动疾病发生。此后，我们发现，导致白血病的多种 MLL 易位涉及 SEC 的多个亚基；SEC 调控转录延伸和暂停释放，其异常调控可致癌。2,3 如本综述所述，我们及同行的研究共同表明，转录延伸控制不仅对发育中的基因表达调控重要，也参与疾病发生及衰老。

Original: In this review, we present a current understanding of the molecular mechanisms involved in RNA Pol II elongation control. These mechanisms include recruitment of elongation factors to RNA Pol II, swapping of elongation factors, allosteric regulation of RNA Pol II processivity and RNA Pol II stabilization. We have discussed the biochemical and mechanistic details of elongation factor function that have recently emerged thanks to the development of advanced techniques such as rapid depletion, nascent RNA-seq, and cryo-EM analysis of protein complex structure. We have also attempted to reconcile several discrepancies between recent in vivo findings and earlier in vitro biochemical evidence regarding promoter-proximal pause release and elongation processivity. Finally, we have highlighted the functional link between dysregulation of transcriptional elongation and various human diseases.

中文: 本综述介绍了目前对 RNA Pol II 延伸调控分子机制的认识，包括延伸因子向 RNA Pol II 的招募、延伸因子交换、RNA Pol II 过程性的变构调控，以及 RNA Pol II 的稳定。快速耗竭、新生 RNA-seq 和蛋白复合物冷冻电镜结构分析等技术的发展，揭示了延伸因子作用的新生化与机制细节。我们还尝试协调近期体内发现与早期体外生化证据之间，关于启动子近端暂停释放及延伸过程性的一些分歧。最后，我们强调了转录延伸失调与多种人类疾病之间的功能联系。

Original: Dysregulated transcriptional elongation has long been associated both with developmental defects and with pathological processes including cancer and infectious disease.Now,accumulating evidence suggests a critical involvement of dysregulated RNA Pol II elongation in the aging process, underscoring the importance of transcriptional elongation control in life. Notably, it has been demonstrated that slowing down the RNA Pol II elongation rate results in extended lifespan in model systems.However, it is entirely unknown how changes to the RNA Pol II rate might affect macro aging processes, whether the function of elongation factors might change over the course of a lifetime, or which specific tissues might play a role in the macro aging processes regulated by RNA Pol II elongation rates. Although aging is also associated with the loss of long transcripts, the existence or direction of the causal relationship between the two remains unclear. Nonetheless, achieving precise control of RNA Pol II dynamics will be crucial to the development of therapeutic approaches to extend lifespan and transcriptional elongation control might be the holy grail for aging. Precise control of the process would require modulation of RNA Pol II rate while also maintaining processivity in difficult sequence environments to avoid significant loss of long transcripts. Future studies that solidify a fundamental understanding of transcriptional elongation regulation are therefore the key to successful development not only of anti-cancer drugs but also of safe and feasible therapies to potentially slow or ease the aging process.

中文: 长期以来，转录延伸失调一直与发育缺陷、癌症和感染性疾病等病理过程有关。如今，越来越多证据提示 RNA Pol II 延伸失调也深度参与衰老，进一步凸显了转录延伸控制在生命过程中的重要性。研究已表明，减慢 RNA Pol II 延伸速率可延长模式系统的寿命。然而，RNA Pol II 速率的变化如何影响机体层面的衰老，延伸因子的功能是否随年龄变化，以及哪些特定组织参与由延伸速率调控的机体衰老过程，目前均不清楚。虽然衰老也伴随长转录本减少，但两者之间是否存在因果关系及其方向仍待确定。尽管如此，精确控制 RNA Pol II 动态，将是开发延寿干预的重要环节；转录延伸控制或许是衰老研究长期追寻的“圣杯”。要实现这种精确控制，需要在调节 RNA Pol II 速率的同时，维持其在难以转录的序列环境中的过程性，以避免长转录本大量丢失。因此，未来对转录延伸调控基本机制的深入研究，不仅是成功开发抗癌药物的关键，也关系到能否开发出安全、可行、可能延缓或减轻衰老的疗法。
