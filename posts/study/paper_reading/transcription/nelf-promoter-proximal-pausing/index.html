NELF Regulates a Promoter-Proximal Step Distinct from RNA Pol II Pause-Release#

NELF 调控与 RNA 聚合酶 II 暂停-释放不同的启动子附近步骤

In Brief｜简要介绍#

Metazoan RNA Pol II-transcribed genes exhibit post-initiation regulation called pausing. Aoi et al. find that loss of the protein complex thought to maintain pausing, NELF, does not result in global release of RNA Pol II but instead may regulate other promoter-proximal regulatory steps such as 5′ mRNA cap stability.

后生动物 RNA 聚合酶II转录基因表现出一种称为暂停的转录起始后调控机制。Aoi 等人发现，被认为维持暂停状态的蛋白质复合物 NELF 的缺失并不会导致 RNA 聚合酶 II 的全局释放，而是可能调节其他启动子附近的调控步骤，例如 5’ mRNA 帽结构的稳定性。

Highlights｜主要发现#

  • Acute NELF depletion reveals 2-step pausing of RNA Pol II at promoters.

  • The 1st-to-2nd pausing transition is independent of P-TEFb/SEC activity.

  • The heat shock response remains intact in the absence of NELF.

  • NELF recruits the cap-binding complex with modest effects on 5′ cap stability.

  • 急性耗竭 NELF 揭示启动子处 RNA Pol II 的两步暂停。

  • 从第一暂停区域向第二暂停区域的转移不依赖 P-TEFb/SEC 活性。

  • 缺失 NELF 时，热休克反应仍保持完整。

  • NELF 招募帽结合复合物，对 5′ 帽稳定性的影响较为有限。

SUMMARY｜摘要#

RNA polymerase II (RNA Pol II) is generally paused at promoter-proximal regions in most metazoans, and based on in vitro studies, this function has been attributed to the negative elongation factor (NELF). Here, we show that upon rapid depletion of NELF, RNA Pol II fails to be released into gene bodies, stopping instead around the +1 nucleosomal dyad-associated region. The transition to the 2nd pause region is independent of positive transcription elongation factor P-TEFb. During the heat shock response, RNA Pol II is rapidly released from pausing at heat shock-induced genes, while most genes are paused and transcriptionally downregulated. Both of these aspects of the heat shock response remain intact upon NELF loss. We find that NELF depletion results in global loss of cap-binding complex from chromatin without global reduction of nascent transcript 5′ cap stability. Thus, our studies implicate NELF functioning in early elongation complexes distinct from RNA Pol II pause-release.

在大多数后生动物中，RNA 聚合酶 II (RNA Pol II) 通常在启动子附近区域暂停，根据体外研究，这一功能被认为是由负向延伸因子 (NELF) 所介导。在此，我们显示，在快速耗尽 NELF 后，RNA Pol II 无法进入基因体，而是在 +1 核小体 dyad 相关区域停止。向第二个暂停区域的转换与正向转录延伸因子 P-TEFb 无关。在热休克反应期间，RNA Pol II 快速从热休克诱导基因的暂停状态中释放出来，而大多数基因则处于暂停状态并被转录下调。在 NELF 缺失的情况下，热休克反应的这两个方面均保持完整。我们发现，NELF 耗尽导致帽结合复合物从染色质上全局性丢失，但新生转录本 5′ 帽结构稳定性并未全局降低。因此，我们的研究指出了 NELF 在不同于 RNA Pol II 暂停-释放的早期延伸复合物中的功能。

INTRODUCTION｜引言#

In most metazoans, most genes exhibit RNA polymerase II (RNA Pol II) pausing at promoter-proximal regions. Release of the paused RNA Pol II into the productive elongation state is thought to be a key regulatory step in response to environmental and developmental signals, while misregulation of RNA Pol II pause-release is associated with oncogenesis such as pediatric leukemia and MYC-driven solid tumors (Chen et al., 2018). Using in vitro reconstitution assays of transcription and in vivo screens, many studies have identified protein complexes required for RNA Pol II pausing, including negative elongation factor (NELF) (Wada et al., 1998), DRB sensitivity-inducible factor (DSIF) (Yamaguchi et al., 1999), RNA Pol II-associated factor 1 (PAF1) (Chen et al., 2015a, 2017), and even the nucleosome, the basic structural unit of chromatin. Nucleosomes can serve as a molecular barrier for RNA Pol II transcription (Kujirai et al., 2018; Lai and Pugh, 2017; Weber et al., 2014). Positive elongation factor b (P-TEFb), which is a complex of cyclin-dependent kinase (CDK) 9 and Cyclin T, is required for release of RNA Pol II from pausing (Marshall and Price, 1995). P-TEFb is catalytically activated by the super elongation complex (SEC) (Lin et al., 2010) or BRD4 (Jang et al., 2005; Yang et al., 2005) but inactivated by the HEXIM-7SK small nuclear ribonucleoprotein particle (snRNP) complex (Nguyen et al., 2001; Yang et al., 2001). How these factors and the nucleosome work together or separately to regulate promoter-proximal pausing of RNA Pol II remains poorly understood.

在大多数后生动物中，大多数基因在启动子附近区域表现出RNA聚合酶II（RNA Pol II）暂停。释放暂停的RNA Pol II进入有效的延伸状态被认为是响应环境和发育信号的关键调节步骤，而RNA Pol II暂停-释放的失调与致癌作用相关，例如儿童白血病和MYC驱动的实体瘤（Chen等，2018）。通过体外转录重建实验和体内筛选，许多研究已经鉴定出维持RNA Pol II暂停所需的蛋白质复合物，包括负延伸因子（NELF）（Wada等，1998），DRB敏感性诱导因子（DSIF）（Yamaguchi等，1999），RNA Pol II相关因子1（PAF1）（Chen等，2015a, 2017），甚至核小体，染色质的基本结构单位。核小体可以作为RNA Pol II转录的分子屏障（Kujirai等，2018；Lai和Pugh，2017；Weber等，2014）。正延伸因子b（P-TEFb）是一个由细胞周期依赖性激酶（CDK）9和Cyclin T组成的复合物，对于释放RNA Pol II的暂停状态是必需的（Marshall和Price，1995）。P-TEFb通过超级延伸复合物（SEC）（Lin等，2010）或BRD4（Jang等，2005；Yang等，2005）被催化激活，但被HEXIM-7SK小核核糖核蛋白颗粒（snRNP）复合物（Nguyen等，2001；Yang等，2001）失活。这些因子和核小体如何共同或单独调节RNA Pol II在启动子附近区域的暂停仍然不甚了解。

5′ Capping of mRNA｜mRNA的5’帽结构#

Despite the pervasiveness of promoter-proximal pausing in metazoans and the abundant evidence that this is a highly regulated process, why this regulatory step is needed is still debated. Although polymerase poised at the promoter waiting for an activation signal was thought to facilitate rapid transcriptional response (Nechaev and Adelman, 2011), rapid transcription can occur in the absence of promoter-proximal pausing (Lin et al., 2011). Pausing has also been proposed to facilitate synchronous gene activation in response to developmental cues (Boettiger and Levine, 2009; Lin et al., 2011). Another idea is that promoter-proximal pausing acts as a checkpoint for cotranscriptional processes, such as to ensure mRNA capping (Sims et al., 2004). Eukaryotic mRNA contains a conserved 5′ m7G cap modification, thereby ensuring mRNA integrity partially through prevention of RNA degradation from its 5′ end (Ramanathan et al., 2016). Capping takes place cotranscriptionally upon �20 nt RNA synthesis by RNA Pol II in vitro and in vivo, indicating that the 5′ end of nascent RNA can be capped when it emerges from the RNA exit tunnel of RNA Pol II (Mandal et al., 2004; Martinez-Rucobo et al., 2015; Rasmussen and Lis, 1993). RNA Pol II is paused at promoter-proximal regions after transcribing 20–120 nt RNA (Chen et al., 2018). These studies have suggested that most nascent RNA that is associated with paused RNA Pol II is capped. Decapping enzymes can be localized to promoters, along with the 5′-3′ RNA exonuclease XRN2, which has been proposed to function as a torpedo for promoterproximal termination (Brannan et al., 2012), suggesting that the 5′ cap modification can be reversible at promoters. The 5′ cap is further protected by the cap-binding complex (CBC) (Ramanathan et al., 2016). Interestingly, NELF has been shown to directly bind to CBC and to regulate 3′ end processing for histone mRNAs (Narita et al., 2007; Schulze and Cusack, 2017). However, a connection between CBC and promoter-proximal pausing has not been investigated.

尽管后生动物中启动子附近区域的暂停普遍存在，并且有大量证据表明这是一个高度调控的过程，但为何需要这一调节步骤仍存在争议。虽然认为在启动子处等待激活信号的聚合酶有助于快速转录反应（Nechaev和Adelman，2011），但在没有启动子附近暂停的情况下也可以发生快速转录（Lin等，2011）。暂停也被提出可以促进对发育线索的同步基因激活（Boettiger和Levine，2009；Lin等，2011）。另一种观点是，启动子附近暂停作为共转录过程的检查点，例如确保mRNA帽结构的形成（Sims等，2004）。真核生物的mRNA含有保守的5’ m7G帽修饰，从而通过防止从其5’端的RNA降解部分保证mRNA的完整性（Ramanathan等，2016）。帽结构在RNA Pol II体外和体内转录约20 nt RNA后共转录地发生，表明当新生RNA从RNA Pol II的RNA出口隧道中出现时，其5’端可以被帽化（Mandal等，2004；Martinez-Rucobo等，2015；Rasmussen和Lis，1993）。在转录20–120 nt RNA后，RNA Pol II在启动子附近区域暂停（Chen等，2018）。这些研究表明，与暂停的RNA Pol II相关的大多数新生RNA都被帽化。脱帽酶可以定位于启动子，连同5’–3’ RNA外切酶XRN2，后者被认为作为启动子附近终止的鱼雷发挥作用（Brannan等，2012），这表明在启动子处帽修饰可能是可逆的。帽结构进一步被帽结合复合物（CBC）保护（）。（Ramanathan等，2016）。有趣的是，NELF已被证明可以直接结合CBC并调节组蛋白mRNA的3’端加工（Narita等，2007；Schulze和Cusack，2017）。然而，CBC与启动子附近暂停之间的联系尚未被研究。

Mechanism of Pausing Regulation by NELF In Vitro and In Vivo｜NELF调控暂停的机制：体外与体内#

An inhibitory function of NELF for RNA Pol II transcription has been extensively studied in vitro using reconstituted transcription systems. NELF was initially isolated from human nuclear extract as a complex that prevents RNA Pol II transcription on a naked DNA template in vitro (Yamaguchi et al., 1999). Four distinct subunits, NELF-A, NELF-B, NELF-C/D (alternative translation isoforms of the NELFCD gene), and NELF-E, form the NELF complex, which is conserved among most metazoans, including human, mouse, and fly (Narita et al., 2003; Vos et al., 2016; Wu et al., 2005). The cryo-electron microscopy structure of RNA Pol II with NELF and DSIF has further suggested how NELF tentacles could restrain RNA Pol II mobility (Vos et al., 2018b).

NELF对RNA聚合酶II转录的抑制功能已在体外使用重建的转录系统中得到广泛研究。NELF最初是从人核提取物中分离出的一个复合物，该复合物可在裸露的DNA模板上抑制RNA聚合酶II的转录（Yamaguchi等，1999）。四个不同的亚基，即NELF-A、NELF-B、NELF-C/D（NELFCD基因的替代翻译异构体）和NELF-E，构成了NELF复合物。该复合物在大多数后生动物中是保守的，包括人类、小鼠和果蝇（Narita等，2003；Vos等，2016；Wu等，2005）。冷冻电子显微镜下RNA聚合酶II与NELF和DSIF的结构进一步揭示了NELF的“触手”如何限制RNA聚合酶II的移动（Vos等，2018b）。”

NELF is proposed to be required for the establishment of pausing by RNA Pol II for key biological events, including the heat shock response (Wu et al., 2003), estrogen signaling (Aiyar et al., 2004), interleukin-6 (IL-6) signaling (Aida et al., 2006), the inflammatory response (Adelman et al., 2009), fibroblast growth factor (FGF)/extracellular signal-regulated kinases (ERK) signaling (Williams et al., 2015), cell growth (Sun et al., 2011; Williams et al., 2015), and early embryonic development (Amleh et al., 2009; Williams et al., 2015), thus highlighting the physiological significance of NELF. However, reconciling in vitro studies of NELF with in vivo studies has been challenging. Unlike an essential role of NELF for pausing in vitro, NELF-depleted cells exhibited limited reduction of paused RNA Pol II at promoters, with few genes upregulated and many genes downregulated at the mRNA level, and reduced RNA Pol II occupancy in the gene body of many genes (Chen et al., 2015b; Core et al., 2012; Gilchrist et al., 2008, 2010; Muse et al., 2007; Rahl et al., 2010; Stadelmayer et al., 2014; Sun and Li, 2010; Sun et al., 2011; Williams et al., 2015; Yamamoto et al., 2014). These results have revealed a significant gap in our understanding of in vitro and in vivo pausing.

据推测，NELF对于RNA聚合酶II在关键生物事件中建立暂停是必需的，包括热休克反应（Wu等，2003）、雌激素信号传导（Aiyar等，2004）、白细胞介素-6（IL-6）信号传导（Aida等，2006）、炎症反应（Adelman等，2009）、成纤维细胞生长因子（FGF）/细胞外信号调节激酶（ERK）信号传导（Williams等，2015）、细胞生长（Sun等，2011；Williams等，2015）以及早期胚胎发育（Amleh等，2009；Williams等，2015），从而突显了NELF的生理学意义。然而，将体外对NELF的研究与体内研究相协调一直具有挑战性。不同于NELF在体外对暂停的必要作用，NELF耗竭细胞中启动子附近暂停的RNA聚合酶II仅出现有限减少，少数基因在mRNA水平上上调，而许多基因下调，并且许多基因的基因体中RNA聚合酶II的占据显著减少（Chen等，2015b；Core等，2012；Gilchrist等，2008, 2010；Muse等，2007；Rahl等，2010；Stadelmayer等，2014；Sun和Li，2010；Sun等，2011；Williams等，2015；Yamamoto等，2014）。这些结果揭示了我们对体外与体内暂停机制理解中的显著差距。”

The recent emergence of rapid degradation strategies holds great promise for dissecting cellular mechanisms. Therefore, to examine NELF functions on RNA Pol II pausing in human cells, we have established rapid NELF depletion in cells using the auxin-inducible degron (AID) (Natsume et al., 2016). We find that promoter-proximal pausing is largely intact in the absence of NELF, leading us to propose that NELF functions at a promoter-proximal step distinct from regulating the release of RNA Pol II from pausing.

近期快速降解策略的出现为解析细胞机制带来了巨大希望。因此，为了在人类细胞中研究NELF对RNA聚合酶II暂停的功能，我们利用生长素诱导降解子（AID）建立了细胞中快速的NELF耗竭方法（Natsume等，2016）。我们发现，在缺乏NELF的情况下，启动子附近的暂停基本保持完整，这促使我们提出NELF在启动子附近的一个不同于调控RNA聚合酶II从暂停状态释放的步骤中发挥作用。”

We employed the AID system (Natsume et al., 2016) to rapidly deplete cells of NELF. The endogenous NELFC gene was tagged with the mini-AID cassette at its C terminus in human DLD-1 cells that constitutively express the AID adaptor protein OsTIR1 (Figure 1A; STAR Methods). �90% of NELF-C-AID protein disappeared within 1 h of auxin treatment in NELF-C-AID cells (Figure 1A). NELF-C protein was stable in auxin-treated parental cells (Figure 1A), confirming that NELF-C-AID is degraded in an AID-tag-dependent manner. Notably, the NELF-E subunit was stable for up to 4 h of auxin treatment in NELF-C-AID cells, but it began to disappear at later time points (Figure 1A). These interdependences of NELF subunits for stability are consistent with previous studies (Narita et al., 2007; Sun et al., 2008; Williams et al., 2015). Long-term NELF-C-AID loss led to a severe growth defect (Figure 1B), which could lead to indirect effects on transcription. Therefore, we chose % 4 h of auxin treatment for further experiments to ensure loss of NELF while minimizing indirect effects of NELF loss on RNA Pol II transcription.

我们采用了AID系统（Natsume等，2016）来快速耗竭细胞中的NELF。在持续表达AID适配蛋白OsTIR1的人类DLD-1细胞中，内源性NELFC基因在其C端被mini-AID盒标记（图1A；STAR方法）。在NELF-C-AID细胞中，生长素处理1小时后，90%的NELF-C-AID蛋白消失（图1A）。在生长素处理的亲本细胞中，NELF-C蛋白稳定（图1A），证实了NELF-C-AID是以AID标签依赖的方式被降解。值得注意的是，在NELF-C-AID细胞中，NELF-E亚基在生长素处理4小时内保持稳定，但在较晚时间点开始消失（图1A）。这些NELF亚基之间对稳定性的相互依赖性与之前的研究一致（Narita等，2007；Sun等，2008；Williams等，2015）。长期缺失NELF-C-AID导致严重的生长缺陷（图1B），这可能导致对转录的间接影响。因此，我们选择了4小时的生长素处理进行进一步实验，以确保缺失NELF的同时尽量减少NELF缺失对RNA聚合酶II转录的间接影响。

Loss of NELF Complex on Chromatin upon NELF-C-AID Depletion｜NELF-C-AID耗竭后染色质上NELF复合物的丢失#

We carried out chromatin immunoprecipitation sequencing (ChIP-seq) experiments to test whether NELF-C-AID degradation reduces NELF-C-AID protein on chromatin. In the absence of auxin, ChIP-seq for NELF-C, NELF-E, and a DSIF subunit, SPT5, showed that their peaks were colocalized at promoter regions (Figures 1C and S1A). This result indicates that the AID-tagged NELF-C protein is properly incorporated into the NELF-DSIF-Pol II pausing complex at promoters. When NELFC-AID cells were treated with auxin for 2 h, NELF-C ChIP-seq peaks disappeared (Figure 1C). NELF-E ChIP-seq peaks simultaneously disappeared upon NELF-C-AID degradation (Figure 1C), despite NELF-E still being present in cells at this time point (Figure 1A). These results indicate that depletion of NELF-C-AID results in the loss of the NELF complex on chromatin. However, SPT5 ChIP-seq peaks largely remained upon NELF loss (Figure 1C), consistent with the NELF-independent binding of SPT5 to RNA Pol II seen in vitro (Missra and Gilmour, 2010; Yamaguchi et al., 2002). Genome-wide analysis of ChIPseq data showed global reduction of NELF-C-AID and NELF-E occupancy at RNA Pol II pause regions upon auxin treatment, while SPT5 largely remained (Figure 1D). Altogether, our data indicate that rapid NELF-C-AID degradation leads to loss of the NELF complex from chromatin.

我们进行了染色质免疫沉淀测序（ChIP-seq）实验，以检测NELF-C-AID降解是否会导致NELF-C-AID蛋白在染色质上的减少。在没有生长素的情况下，针对NELF-C、NELF-E和DSIF亚基SPT5的ChIP-seq显示，它们的峰值在启动子区域共定位（图1C和S1A）。这一结果表明，AID标记的NELF-C蛋白被正确地整合到启动子附近的NELF-DSIF-RNA聚合酶II暂停复合物中。当NELFC-AID细胞用生长素处理2 h后，NELF-C ChIP-seq峰值消失（图1C）。尽管在该时间点NELF-E仍存在于细胞中（图1A），但在NELF-C-AID降解的同时，NELF-E ChIP-seq峰值也消失了（图1C）。这些结果表明，NELF-C-AID的耗竭导致了染色质上NELF复合物的丢失。然而，在失去NELF的情况下，SPT5 ChIP-seq峰值大部分仍然存在（图1C），这与体外观察到的SPT5独立于NELF结合RNA聚合酶II的现象一致（Missra和Gilmour, 2010；Yamaguchi等, 2002）。对ChIPseq数据进行全基因组分析显示，在生长素处理后，NELF-C-AID和NELF-E在RNA聚合酶II暂停区域的占据显著减少，而SPT5则基本保持不变（图1D）。综上所述，我们的数据表明，快速的NELF-C-AID降解导致了NELF复合物从染色质上的丢失。

To precisely explore functions of NELF in RNA Pol II transcription, we determined the genome-wide distribution of active RNA Pol II with precision nuclear run-on sequencing (PRO-seq) (Kwak et al., 2013). PRO-seq track examples show that transcriptionally active RNA Pol II is paused at the promoter regions in the untreated condition (Figure 2A). The distribution of RNA Pol II shows a modest shift downstream at 1 and 4 h of auxin treatment in NELF-C-AID cells (Figure 2A). To analyze this RNA Pol II shift genome-wide, we defined the original pause region at each active gene: the nucleotide with the maximum PRO-seq signal at the promoter-proximal region in untreated NELF-C-AID cells was defined as the location of the 1st pause region, while the maximum in NELF-C-AID cells treated with auxin for 1 h was defined as the location of the 2nd pause region (see STAR Methods). Metaplot analysis of the PRO-seq signal centered on 1st pause regions showed that the PRO-seq signal at 1st pause regions decreased upon NELF loss, whereas an increased PRO-seq peak corresponding to the 2nd pause region occurred around 50 bp downstream of the 1st pause region upon NELF loss (Figure 2B). The histogram showed that the median distance between the 1st and the 2nd pause regions is 49 bp and most (81.7%) transcribing genes have a distance no longer than 150 bp (Figure 2C). These results suggest that RNA Pol II undergoes 2-step pausing and NELF loss results in transitioning of paused RNA Pol II to 2nd pause regions.

为了精确探讨NELF在RNA聚合酶II转录中的功能，我们使用精确核运行测序（PRO-seq）确定了活性RNA聚合酶II的全基因组分布（Kwak等, 2013）。PRO-seq轨迹示例显示，在未处理条件下，转录活跃的RNA聚合酶II在启动子区域处于暂停状态（图2A）。在NELF-C-AID细胞中，生长素处理1 h和4 h后，RNA聚合酶II的分布显示出适度的下游偏移（图2A）。为了对这一RNA聚合酶II的偏移进行全基因组分析，我们定义了每个活跃基因的原始暂停区域：在未处理的NELF-C-AID细胞中，启动子附近区域PRO-seq信号最大的核苷酸被定义为第一暂停区域的位置；而在生长素处理1 h后的NELF-C-AID细胞中，PRO-seq信号最大的位置则被定义为第二暂停区域的位置（见 STAR Methods）。以第一个暂停区域为中心的 PRO-seq 信号元图谱分析显示，在 NELF 缺失时，第一个暂停区域的 PRO-seq 信号减少，而在 NELF 缺失时，对应于第二个暂停区域的 PRO-seq 峰出现在第一个暂停区域下游约 50 bp 处（图 2B）。直方图显示第一个和第二个暂停区域之间的中位距离为 49 bp，且大多数（81.7%）转录基因的距离不超过 150 bp（图 2C）。这些结果表明 RNA 聚合酶II 经历两步暂停，而 NELF 缺失导致暂停的 RNA 聚合酶II 转移到第二个暂停区域。

To test whether NELF loss leads to release of paused RNA Pol II to productive elongation, we generated heatmaps of PRO-seq log2 fold change at gene bodies. NELF-C ChIP-seq peaks overlapped 1st pause regions (Figure 2D), confirming that the RNA Pol II-NELF pausing complex occupies 1st pause regions. Upon NELF loss at 1 and 4 h of auxin treatment, the PRO-seq signal in gene bodies was uniformly and significantly decreased (Figures 2D and 2E). The PRO-seq signal was significantly decreased at 1st pause regions and significantly increased at 2nd pause regions upon NELF loss (Figures 2D and 2E). These data suggest that the paused RNA Pol II at 2nd pause regions is not able to enter productive elongation despite NELF loss.

为了测试 NELF 缺失是否导致暂停的 RNA 聚合酶II 进入生产性延伸，我们生成了基因体中 PRO-seq 对数倍变化的热图。NELF-C ChIP-seq 峰与第一个暂停区域重叠（图 2D），确认 RNA 聚合酶II-NELF 暂停复合物占据第一个暂停区域。在生长素处理 1 和 4 h 后 NELF 缺失时，基因体中的 PRO-seq 信号均匀且显著减少（图 2D 和 2E）。在 NELF 缺失时，第一个暂停区域的 PRO-seq 信号显著减少，而第二个暂停区域的 PRO-seq 峰显著增加（图 2D 和 2E）。这些数据表明，尽管 NELF 缺失，第二个暂停区域的暂停 RNA 聚合酶II 仍无法进入生产性延伸。

Given this observation, we also examined the effects of longterm depletion of NELF. At 24 h of auxin treatment, the PRO-seq signal was significantly decreased at promoters and gene bodies. Given the discrepancy between the results with NELF loss within 1 to 4 h and those within 24 h, we propose that the global loss of the PRO-seq signal throughout active genes after 24 h of NELF loss may result from indirect effects of NELF loss, because we observed a severe growth defect at this time point. The effect of 24 h NELF depletion with the AID system closely resembles what has been seen in studies depleting NELF with short hairpin RNA (shRNA) treatment over several days (Sun and Li, 2010).

鉴于这一观察结果，我们还研究了 NELF 长期耗竭的影响。在生长素处理 24 h 后，启动子和基因体中的 PRO-seq 信号显著减少。鉴于在 1 至 4 h 内 NELF 缺失与 24 h 内 NELF 缺失结果之间的差异，我们提出在 NELF 缺失 24 h 后整个活跃基因中 PRO-seq 信号的全局丢失可能是由 NELF 缺失的间接效应引起的，因为在此时间点我们观察到严重的生长缺陷。使用 AID 系统进行 24 h NELF 耗竭的效果与用短发夹 RNA（shRNA）处理数天后 NELF 耗竭研究中所见的结果非常相似（Sun 和 Li, 2010）。

NELF-E-AID Depletion Also Showed 2-Step Pausing｜NELF-E-AID 的耗竭也显示了两步暂停#

To test the effects of depleting another NELF subunit, we generated NELF-E-AID cells. Western blotting showed that R80% of NELF-E-AID proteins were degraded within 1 h of auxin treatment (Figure S1B). ChIP-seq for NELF-C, NELF-E, and SPT5 in NELF-E-AID cells showed that NELF-E-AID degradation resulted in loss of NELF-C and NELF-E peaks, while SPT5 peaks were not reduced as much as NELF subunits (Figures S1C and S1D). Metaplot analysis of PRO-seq showed accumulation of the PRO-seq signal around 50 bp downstream of 1st pause regions (Figure S2A). These results suggest that NELF-E-AID degradation leads to loss of the NELF complex on chromatin and leads to RNA Pol II accumulation at 2nd pause regions, consistent with results in NELF-C-AID cells. Importantly, parental DLD-1 cells did not display PRO-seq signal accumulation downstream of 1st pause regions upon auxin treatment (Figure S2B), excluding the possibility that this effect results from an off-target effect of auxin treatment causing RNA Pol II to accumulate at 2nd pause regions.

为了测试耗竭另一种 NELF 亚基的效果，我们生成了 NELF-E-AID 细胞。Western blotting 显示，在生长素处理后 1 小时内，约 80% 的 NELF-E-AID 蛋白质被降解（图 S1B）。在 NELF-E-AID 细胞中对 NELF-C、NELF-E 和 SPT5 进行的 ChIP-seq 显示，NELF-E-AID 的降解导致了 NELF-C 和 NELF-E 峰的丢失，而 SPT5 峰并未像 NELF 亚基那样显著减少（图 S1C 和 S1D）。PRO-seq 的 metaplot 分析显示，在第一个暂停区域下游约 50 bp 处累积了 PRO-seq 信号（图 S2A）。这些结果表明，NELF-E-AID 降解导致染色质上 NELF 复合物的丢失，并导致 RNA 聚合酶 II 在第二暂停区域的累积，这与 NELF-C-AID 细胞中的结果一致。重要的是，亲本 DLD-1 细胞在生长素处理后并未在第一暂停区域下游显示出 PRO-seq 信号的累积（图 S2B），排除了这种效应是由生长素处理引起的脱靶效应导致 RNA 聚合酶 II 在第二暂停区域累积的可能性。

The phosphorylation states of the RNA Pol II C-terminal domain (CTD) have been shown to regulate RNA Pol II throughout the transcription cycle by a series of CDKs, including initiation, when CDK7 phosphorylates the CTD on serine 5, and promoter-proximal pause-release, which is associated with serine 2 phosphorylation by CDK9-Cyclin T (P-TEFb) (Chen et al., 2018). Accordingly, phospho-Ser5 is enriched at promoters, while phospho-Ser2 occupancy increases along gene bodies. We tested whether NELF loss affects CTD phosphorylation states. Metaplot analysis of ChIP-seq for phospho-Ser2 and phospho-Ser5 of RNA Pol II CTD showed that both phosphorylation levels at promoters were maintained upon NELF loss. Notably, these phosphorylation peaks shifted slightly downstream of pause regions (Figure 4A), corresponding to the transition of paused RNA Pol II to 2nd pause regions. These results suggest that NELF loss does not result in alteration of CTD phosphorylation states and therefore that the transition of paused RNA Pol II to the 2nd pause region can occur without changes in CTD phosphorylation.

RNA聚合酶II C端结构域（CTD）的磷酸化状态已被证明通过一系列CDKs调控整个转录周期中的RNA聚合酶II，包括起始阶段，此时CDK7在丝氨酸5上磷酸化CTD，以及启动子附近暂停-释放阶段，这与CDK9-Cyclin T（P-TEFb）介导的丝氨酸2磷酸化相关（Chen等，2018）。因此，磷酸化丝氨酸5在启动子处富集，而磷酸化丝氨酸2的占据程度沿基因体增加。我们检测了NELF缺失是否影响CTD磷酸化状态。对RNA聚合酶II CTD磷酸化丝氨酸2和丝氨酸5的ChIP-seq进行元图分析显示，在NELF缺失后，启动子处的两种磷酸化水平均得以维持。值得注意的是，这些磷酸化峰值略微向下游移动至暂停区域（图4A），对应于暂停RNA聚合酶II向第2次暂停区域的转变。这些结果表明，NELF缺失不会导致CTD磷酸化状态的改变，因此暂停的RNA聚合酶II向第2次暂停区域的转变可以在不改变CTD磷酸化的情况下发生。

The Transition of Paused RNA Pol II to the +1 Dyad Is Independent of P-TEFb Activity｜暂停的RNA聚合酶II向+1 核小体二重对称轴的转变独立于P-TEFb活性#

Release from promoter-proximal pausing relies on the activity of the Cdk9-Cyclin T complex known as P-TEFb and may involve phosphorylation of DSIF and NELF, in addition to Ser2 of the RNA Pol II CTD (Chen et al., 2018). Given that NELF loss does not affect phospho-Ser2 levels, we examined whether the transition of paused RNA Pol II to 2nd pause regions depends on P-TEFb activity. We treated NELF-C-AID cells with the P-TEFb inhibitor flavopiridol (FP), followed by auxin treatment to deplete NELF (Figure 4B). Heatmaps of PRO-seq log2 fold change showed that FP treatment greatly induced pausing at 1st pause regions when NELF was present (Figure 4C). Depleting NELF with auxin treatment canceled the FP-induced 1st region of pausing, indicating that transition of paused RNA Pol II upon NELF loss is independent of P-TEFb (Figure 4C). To examine 2nd pause positions at the +1 nucleosome, we generated PRO-seq metaplots centered on the +1 dyad as described for Figure 3C. The metaplot analysis showed that FP-induced pausing occurred at the entrance of the +1 nucleosome, while PRO-seq peaks in the FP + auxin condition were observed at the region between 1st pause regions and the +1 dyad, similar to PRO-seq peaks in the auxin-alone condition (Figure 4D). Because there was no increase in RNA Pol II occupancy at the 2nd pause region after FP treatment in the absence of NELF, this suggests that NELF is generally needed for the establishment of pausing. To exclude potential off-target effects of FP, we also tested the effects of NVP-2, a highly selective P-TEFb inhibitor with sub-nanomolar potency (Olson et al., 2018). Consistent with our results with FP treatment, NVP-2-induced pausing occurred at the 1st pause region in the presence of NELF and shifted to the 2nd pause region upon NELF depletion (Figure S4). These data demonstrate that RNA Pol II can be paused at the entrance of the +1 nucleosome in a NELF-dependent manner (Figure 4E(a)) and that RNA Pol II can resume transcription toward the +1 dyad upon NELF loss without P-TEFb activity (Figure 4E(b)) (see Discussion).

In mammalian cells, the heat shock response has been demonstrated to result in the upregulation of a small subset of genes through release from the paused state while downregulating most genes through increased promoter-proximal pausing (Chen et al., 2017; Mahat et al., 2016a). To test whether acute loss of NELF is required for the heat shock response, we treated NELF-C-AID cells with auxin for 2 h, followed by heat shock treatment at 42� C for 1 h (Figure 5A). We defined heat shock-upregulated genes and heat shock-downregulated genes based on PRO-seq density at gene bodies (see STAR Methods).

在哺乳动物细胞中，热休克反应已被证明通过从暂停状态中释放来上调一小部分基因，而通过增加启动子附近的暂停来下调大多数基因（Chen等，2017；Mahat等，2016a）。为了测试NELF的急性缺失是否是热休克反应所必需的，我们对NELF-C-AID细胞进行2小时的生长素处理，随后在42°C下进行1小时的热休克处理（图5A）。我们根据基因体上的PRO-seq密度定义了热休克上调基因和热休克下调基因（见STAR方法）。

PRO-seq tracks demonstrate that transcriptional upregulation upon heat shock appeared normal in the absence of NELF (Figure S5A). Genome-wide analysis of heat shock-upregulated genes showed that PRO-seq density in gene bodies at 42� C in the absence of NELF is not significantly different from what is seen in the presence of NELF (Figures 5B and 5C). Next, we addressed the significance of NELF-dependent pausing for nascent RNA stability. ChIP-seq peak analysis showed that most CBP80 peaks highly overlap NELF-C peaks (Figure 6A). Annotation of ChIP-seq peaks demonstrated that CBP80 and NELF co-occupy promoters, while NELF peaks without CBP80 peaks are enriched in intron and intergenic regions (Figure S6A). DISCUSSION｜讨论#

The Function of the NELF-CBC Interaction｜NELF-CBC 相互作用的功能#

Using rapid depletion of NELF, we have dissected in vivo functions of NELF in human cells. NELF loss substantially reduces CBC, alters cap stability, and increases occupancies of XRN2 and DCP2 at TSSs. Here, we propose that NELF has primary roles in modulating cap stability through CBC recruitment, which sets up a regulatory mechanism for promoter-proximal early termination of RNA Pol II (Figure 7E, discussed later).

通过快速消耗NELF，我们解析了NELF在人类细胞中体内功能。NELF缺失显著降低了CBC，改变了帽结构稳定性，并增加了XRN2和DCP2在启动子附近区域（TSSs）的占据。在此，我们提出NELF主要通过CBC募集来调节帽结构稳定性，从而建立一种调控机制，用于启动子附近早期终止RNA聚合酶II（见图7E，后续讨论）。

Following NELF depletion, we found a decreased PRO-cap signal at a subset of TSSs that also exhibited a decreased PRO-seq signal, rather than the typical transitioning to a 2nd pause region (Figure 7, cap-down TSSs). This raises the possibility that these transcripts were susceptible to early termination: at these TSSs, loss of CBC, and increased DCP2, may promote the removal of unprotected 5′ cap, and increased XRN2 may stimulate 5′-3′ exonucleolytic degradation, which could lead to early termination of RNA Pol II by the torpedo mechanism (Proudfoot, 2016). Because XRN2 coimmunoprecipitates with DCP2 (Brannan et al., 2012), their recruitment upon NELF loss may be cooperatively regulated.

在NELF消耗后，我们发现一部分TSS处的PRO-cap信号减少，这些TSS同时也表现出PRO-seq信号减少，而不是典型的向第二个暂停区域转变（图7，cap-down TSSs）。这提示这些转录本易发生早期终止：在这些TSS处，CBC缺失以及DCP2增加可能促进未受保护的5’帽结构被移除，而XRN2增加则可能刺激5’-3’外切核酸酶降解，这可能导致RNA聚合酶II通过鱼雷机制（torpedo mechanism）发生早期终止（Proudfoot, 2016）。由于 XRN2 可与 DCP2 共免疫沉淀（Brannan 等，2012），NELF 缺失后两者的招募可能受到协同调控。

However, despite a general increase in DCP2 and XRN2 occupancy after NELF depletion, our PRO-cap analysis showed no corresponding global loss of capping at most TSSs following short periods of NELF depletion. Cap-down TSSs showed a distinct nucleosome profile from TSSs with a 2nd pause region (cap-up TSSs), although we are unable to determine whether this difference is a cause or a consequence of these genes preferentially requiring NELF. It is also not clear why the 5′ cap remains stable at many TSSs even though XRN2 and DCP2 recruitment is increased. Future studies will be needed to better understand the relationship between 5′ cap protection and RNA Pol II pausing.

然而，尽管在NELF耗尽后DCP2和XRN2的占据总体增加，我们的PRO-cap分析显示，在短时间NELF耗尽后，大多数启动子附近转录起始位点（TSS）上并未出现相应的全局帽结构丢失。cap-down TSSs与具有第二暂停区域的TSSs（cap-up TSSs）显示出不同的核小体谱型，尽管我们无法确定这种差异是这些基因优先需要NELF的原因还是结果。同样不清楚的是，为什么在XRN2和DCP2招募增加的情况下，许多TSS上的5’帽结构仍然保持稳定。未来的研究将有助于更好地理解5’帽保护与RNA聚合酶II暂停之间的关系。

Function of Nucleosome-Associated Pausing in the Heat Shock Response｜热休克反应中与核小体相关的暂停功能#

We have found that both upregulation and downregulation of gene expression during the heat shock response are robust in the absence of NELF in human cells (Figure 7). NELF has previously been shown to be dispensable for heat shock-induced gene upregulation in Drosophila cells (Ghosh et al., 2011), which is consistent with our findings. It has also been previously shown that gene downregulation upon heat shock is associated with pausing (Aprile-Garcia et al., 2019; Chen et al., 2017; Mahat et al., 2016a). We found that upon heat shock, RNA Pol II is paused at 1st pause regions for the downregulated genes, consistent with these studies. Importantly, our results have demonstrated that RNA Pol II is also paused at the 2nd pause regions in the absence of NELF (Figure 7), indicating that pausing can be NELF independent, with the canonical region of pausing dependent on NELF pausing.

我们发现，在人类细胞中缺失NELF时，热休克反应期间基因表达的上调和下调均是稳健的（图7）。先前已有研究表明，在果蝇细胞中，NELF对于热休克诱导的基因上调是可有可无的（Ghosh等，2011），这与我们的发现一致。此前还表明，热休克引起的基因下调与暂停相关（Aprile-Garcia等，2019；Chen等，2017；Mahat等，2016a）。我们发现，在热休克下，RNA聚合酶II在下调基因的第1暂停区域发生暂停，这与这些研究一致。重要的是，我们的结果表明，在缺失NELF的情况下，RNA聚合酶II也在第2暂停区域发生暂停（图7），这表明暂停可以不依赖于NELF，而经典的暂停区域则依赖于NELF的暂停。

RNA Pol II Pausing Associated with the +1 Nucleosome｜与+1核小体相关的RNA聚合酶II暂停#

Although the +1 nucleosome is a widely conserved structure of eukaryotic promoters (Lai and Pugh, 2017), whether the +1 nucleosome is required for promoter-proximal pausing in metazoans has not been answered. Cryo-electron microscopy structure studies have shown that transcribing RNA Pol II can be paused at superhelical locations SHL(�6), SHL(�5), SHL(�2), and SHL(�1) of the nucleosome (Kujirai et al., 2018) and that the nucleosome can be accommodated on the NELF-Pol II-DSIF pausing complex (Farnung et al., 2018). Consistent with these studies, our data suggested that RNA Pol II is paused �60 bp upstream of the +1 dyad, which corresponds to SHL(�6), in a NELF-dependent manner. In Drosophila, RNA Pol II pause regions have been shown to be associated with the �60 bp site of the promoter-proximal nucleosome (Weber et al., 2014) or even upstream of the +1 nucleosome (Kwak et al., 2013). Therefore, the RNA Pol II-NELF complex paused around SHL(�6) may be a primary feature of promoter-proximal pausing among metazoans. Future structural analyses could uncover how NELF determines 1st pause regions for RNA Pol II in the context of chromatin.

尽管+1核小体是真核启动子中广泛保守的结构（Lai和Pugh，2017），但在后生动物中+1核小体是否对于启动子附近暂停是必需的尚未得到回答。冷冻电子显微镜结构研究表明，转录中的RNA聚合酶II可以在核小体的超螺旋位置SHL(�6)、SHL(�5)、SHL(�2)和SHL(�1)处暂停（Kujirai等，2018），并且核小体可以容纳在NELF-Pol II-DSIF暂停复合物上（Farnung等，2018）。与这些研究一致，我们的数据表明RNA聚合酶II在+1 核小体二重对称轴上游约60 bp处暂停，对应于SHL(�6)，且依赖于NELF。在果蝇中，已显示RNA聚合酶II暂停区域与启动子附近核小体的-60 bp位点相关（Weber等，2014），甚至位于+1核小体上游（Kwak等，2013）。因此，在后生动物中，RNA聚合酶II-NELF复合物在SHL(�6)处暂停可能是启动子附近暂停的主要特征。未来的结构分析可能会揭示NELF如何在染色质背景下决定RNA聚合酶II的首个暂停区域。

We found that in the absence of NELF, RNA Pol II is found at 2nd pause regions, which are associated with regions between the 1st pause regions and the +1 dyad. These pause regions may be SHL(�5), SHL(�2), and SHL(�1) of the 1st nucleosome.

我们发现，在缺乏NELF的情况下，RNA聚合酶II出现在第二暂停区域，这些区域与第一暂停区域和+1 核小体二重对称轴之间的区域相关。这些暂停区域可能是第一核小体的SHL(�5)、SHL(�2)和SHL(�1)位点。

The elongation factors Elf1 and DSIF have been shown to cooperatively prevent RNA Pol II pausing at SHL(�6), SHL(�5), and SHL(�2) (Ehara et al., 2019). Recruitment of these elongation factors to RNA Pol II may facilitate effective release of paused RNA Pol II into productive elongation. It remains possible that the 2nd pause region is associated with unknown factors other than the nucleosome. Future in vivo studies will determine the mechanistic details for 2nd pausing of RNA Pol II upon NELF loss.

已知延伸因子Elf1和DSIF协同阻止RNA聚合酶II在SHL(�6)、SHL(�5)和SHL(�2)处暂停（Ehara等，2019）。这些延伸因子被招募到RNA聚合酶II上可能有助于有效释放暂停的RNA聚合酶II进入有效的延伸阶段。也有可能第二暂停区域与核小体以外未知因素相关。未来的体内研究将确定在失去NELF后RNA聚合酶II第二暂停的机制细节。

Reconciling In Vitro and In Vivo Roles of NELF｜整合体外和体内NELF的作用#

We find that acute loss of NELF does not release RNA Pol II from promoter-proximal pausing in living cells (Figure 2). Rather, RNA Pol II is found at the +1 dyad-associated regions in the absence of NELF. This means that NELF’s function in CBC recruitment can be functionally distinct from regulation of RNA Pol II pause-release. Consistent with this in vivo finding is that the C-terminal tentacle domain of the NELF-E subunit, which has an RNA recognition motif (RRM) and CBC binding domain, is not required for pausing in vitro (Missra and Gilmour, 2010; Vos et al., 2018b). In vivo, the NELF-E RRM may capture nascent RNA to facilitate its binding to CBC.

我们发现，NELF的急性缺失并不会使RNA聚合酶II从启动子附近的暂停状态中释放出来（图2）。相反，在缺乏NELF的情况下，RNA聚合酶II存在于+1 dyad相关区域。这意味着NELF在帽结合复合物（CBC）招募中的功能可能与调控RNA聚合酶II暂停-释放的功能不同。与此体内发现一致的是，NELF-E亚基的C端伸展结构域，该结构域具有RNA识别基序（RRM）和CBC结合结构域，在体外暂停过程中并非必需（Missra和Gilmour，2010；Vos等，2018b）。在体内，NELF-E RRM可能捕捉新生RNA以促进其与CBC的结合。

The transition of paused RNA Pol II to 2nd pause regions in the absence of NELF occurs in a P-TEFb-independent manner (Figure 4), further demonstrating that NELF functions at a step distinct from pause-release. We reconcile the in vitro requirement of NELF for P-TEFb-dependent pause-release with our in vivo findings as follows. In an initial in vitro transcription system, NELF, when bound to RNA Pol II with DSIF, was shown to possess an intrinsic ability to inhibit RNA Pol II, and this could be relieved by P-TEFb (Yamaguchi et al., 1999). However, when a minimal transcription system with recombinant proteins was used, P-TEFb alone was unable to relieve this RNA Pol II inhibition (Vos et al., 2018a; Wada et al., 2000), suggesting that P-TEFb phosphorylation of NELF, DSIF, and Pol II CTD is not sufficient for RNA Pol II release in vitro. In cells, inhibition of RNA Pol II by NELF and DSIF at the entrance of the +1 nucleosome could allow time for NELF to recruit CBC. CBC binding could in turn help activate P-TEFb (Lenasi et al., 2011). Because P-TEFb phosphorylation of NELF, DSIF, and Pol II CTD was not sufficient for release from pausing in vitro, we propose that P-TEFbdependent phosphorylation of unknown factors could be required for RNA Pol II to traverse the +1 nucleosome into productive elongation.

在缺乏NELF的情况下，暂停的RNA聚合酶II向第二个暂停区域的转变是不依赖P-TEFb的方式进行的（图4），进一步表明NELF的功能步骤与暂停释放不同。我们对体外需要NELF参与P-TEFb依赖性暂停释放的现象与我们的体内发现进行统一解释如下。在最初的体外转录系统中，当NELF与RNA聚合酶II结合并被DSIF所捕获时，显示其具有内在抑制RNA聚合酶II的能力，并且这种抑制可以通过P-TEFb解除（Yamaguchi等，1999）。然而，在使用重组蛋白的最小转录系统中，仅P-TEFb无法解除这种RNA聚合酶II的抑制（Vos等，2018a；Wada等，2000），这表明P-TEFb对NELF、DSIF和RNA聚合酶II C端结构域（CTD）的磷酸化不足以在体外实现RNA聚合酶II的释放。在细胞中，NELF和DSIF在+1核小体入口处对RNA聚合酶II的抑制可能为NELF招募CBC留出时间。CBC结合反过来有助于激活P-TEFb（Lenasi等，2011）。由于P-TEFb对NELF、DSIF和RNA聚合酶II CTD的磷酸化在体外不足以实现暂停释放，我们提出，RNA聚合酶II穿越+1核小体进入有效延伸阶段可能需要P-TEFb依赖性磷酸化的未知因子。

The broad presence of NELF across metazoans raises the possibility that it is part of an added layer of transcriptional regulation needed for these more complex genomes and developmental programs. Recently, another metazoan complex, Integrator, was shown to mediate early termination of gene expression through its intrinsic endonuclease activity (Tatomer et al., 2019) (Elrod et al., 2019). Therefore, multiple early termination mechanisms could be used to achieve greater diversity of gene regulatory mechanisms, especially during critical differentiation decisions. Future studies, including expanding the use of acute depletion techniques, can be used to further explore and deconvolve the numerous gene regulatory mechanisms as they occur in their natural chromatin environment in vivo.

NELF在后生动物中的广泛存在提示它可能是复杂基因组和发育程序所需的额外转录调控层的一部分。最近，另一个后生动物复合物Integrator被证明通过其内在的内切酶活性介导基因表达的早期终止（Tatomer等，2019）（Elrod等，2019）。因此，多种早期终止机制可能被用于实现更广泛的基因调控机制，特别是在关键分化决策期间。未来的研究，包括扩展急性耗竭技术的应用，可用于进一步探索并解析在体内自然染色质环境中发生的众多基因调控机制。

Heat Shock Induction｜热休克诱导#

Heat shock of mammalian cells was performed using �70%–80% confluent DLD-1 cells by adding pre-heated (42� C) conditioned media collected from identically growing cells (Mahat et al., 2016a). The heat shock cells were incubated at 42� C for 1 hour. After washing with PBS, the heat shock and non-heat shock DLD-1 cells were processed for PRO-seq as described below.

哺乳动物细胞的热休克处理通过向70%–80%融合的DLD-1细胞中添加预先加热至42°C的培养基进行，该培养基来源于相同生长条件下的细胞（Mahat等，2016a）。热休克细胞在42°C下孵育1小时。用PBS洗涤后，将热休克和非热休克处理的DLD-1细胞按以下所述方法进行PRO-seq处理。

PRO-seq Library Preparation and Alignment｜PRO-seq文库制备与比对#

PRO-seq was performed according to the previously published protocol (Mahat et al., 2016b) with minor modifications. 10 million DLD-1 nuclei were mixed with 0.5 million spike-in Drosophila S2 cell nuclei. Nuclear run-on assays were performed with 25 mM Biotin-11-ATP/UTP/CTP/GTP (PerkinElmer) for 3 min at 30� C. RNA fragmentation was performed with 0.2 M NaOH for 10 min on ice. Biotin-labeled RNA was purified by streptavidin beads M-280 (Thermo). The 5′ cap removal and triphosphate repair were performed with RppH (NEB), 5′ hydroxyl repair was then performed with PNK (NEB). 5′ and 3′ adaptor ligation was performed with T4 RNA ligase I (NEB). Reverse transcription was performed with SuperScript III (ThermoFisher). cDNA was amplified using Phusion Hot Start II DNA polymerase (ThermoFisher). DNA Libraries were size selected by PippinHT (Sage) or AMpure XP (Beckman Coulter) and sequenced on a NextSeq 500 (Illumina). Low-quality bases and adapters from 3′ ends of reads were removed using cutadapt 1.14 (Martin, 2011) requiring a read length of 16–36 bp. Reads derived from ribosomal RNA were filtered out by mapping reads on human and fly ribosomal DNA. The remaining reads were aligned using bowtie 2.2.6 with —very-sensitive option (Langmead and Salzberg, 2012), to a concatenated genome comprised of human hg19 and fly dm3 assemblies. The 5′ ends of aligned reads with MAPQ R 30 were taken by bedtools genomecov 2.25.0 (Quinlan and Hall, 2010) with -strand and �5 options, and strands of the 5′ ends of reads were then swapped. Read counts were normalized to total reads aligned to the spike-in genome.

PRO-seq按照先前发表的方案（Mahat等，2016b）进行，略有修改。将1000万个DLD-1细胞核与50万个果蝇S2细胞核混合。在30°C下使用25 mM生物素-11-ATP/UTP/CTP/GTP（PerkinElmer）进行3分钟的核运行实验。使用0.2 M NaOH在冰上进行RNA片段化10分钟。生物素标记的RNA通过链霉亲和素磁珠M-280（Thermo）纯化。使用RppH（NEB）进行5’帽去除和三磷酸修复，随后使用PNK（NEB）进行5’羟基修复。使用T4 RNA连接酶I（NEB）进行5’和3’接头连接。使用SuperScript III（ThermoFisher）进行反转录。使用Phusion Hot Start II DNA聚合酶（ThermoFisher）扩增cDNA。DNA文库通过PippinHT（Sage）或AMpure XP（Beckman Coulter）进行大小选择，并在NextSeq 500（Illumina）上测序。使用cutadapt 1.14（Martin，2011）去除读段3’端的低质量碱基和接头，要求读段长度为16–36 bp。通过将读段比对到人类和果蝇核糖体DNA，过滤掉来自核糖体RNA的读段。剩余读段使用bowtie 2.2.6以—very-sensitive选项（Langmead和Salzberg，2012）比对到由人类hg19和果蝇dm3组装构成的拼接基因组。将MAPQ值大于30的读段5’端通过bedtools genomecov 2.25.0（Quinlan和Hall，2010）提取，使用-strand和-5选项，并对读段5’端链进行交换。读段计数标准化为比对到内参基因组的总读段数。

PRO-seq Data Analysis｜PRO-seq数据分析#

Protein coding transcripts were chosen from Ensembl version 75 database by R 3.3.3, according to the previously described protocol (Liang et al., 2018) with minor modifications. Briefly, we selected RefSeq-validated transcripts with TSSs that overlapping NELF-C ChIP-seq peaks. We filtered out transcripts that are % 2 kb long and % 2 kb from the nearest genes. Next, we chose transcripts that have R 2 rpm total PRO-seq coverage at the region between TSS – 100 bp and TSS + 300 bp, using 3 independent PRO-seq data in untreated NELF-C-AID cells. In these selected transcripts, the nucleotide position with maximum PRO-seq coverage in the region between TSS – 100 bp and TSS + 300 bp was assigned to the 1st pause region. Transcripts that have % 0.2 rpm coverage at the 1st pause region nucleotide were excluded. The resulting transcripts with assigned the 1st pause regions were used for further analyses. The nucleotide positions with maximum PRO-seq coverage in 1h auxin-treated NELF-C-AID cells at the region from +10 to +500 bp downstream of the 1st pause region was assigned to the 2nd pause region. For Heat shock experiments, transcripts with R 1.5-fold increase or decrease in PRO-seq coverage at gene bodies (the 1st pause regions +500 to +100 kb or to transcription termination sites) upon heat shock were assigned to HS-upregulated or HS-downregulated genes, respectively. Heatmaps and Molecular Cell 78, 261–274.e1–e5, April 16, 2020 e3 metaplots of PRO-seq signal was generated by deepTools 3.0.0/3.1.1/3.1.2 computeMatrix, plotHeatmap, and plotProfile (Ramı´rez et al., 2016). Log2 fold change of PRO-seq signal at nucleotide resolution was calculated by deepTools 3.0.0/3.1.1/3.1.2 bigwigCompare with pseudocount 0.25 rpm.

根据先前描述的方案（Liang等，2018）并略有修改，从Ensembl版本75数据库中选择蛋白质编码转录本，使用R 3.3.3。简而言之，我们选择了RefSeq验证的转录本，其转录起始位点（TSS）与NELF-C ChIP-seq峰重叠。过滤掉长度超过2 kb且距离最近基因超过2 kb的转录本。接着，我们选择了在未处理的NELF-C-AID细胞中使用3个独立的PRO-seq数据，在TSS – 100 bp至TSS + 300 bp区域总PRO-seq覆盖度大于2 rpm的转录本。在这些选定的转录本中，TSS – 100 bp至TSS + 300 bp区域内PRO-seq覆盖度最大的核苷酸位置被指定为第1个暂停区域。排除在第1个暂停区域核苷酸处覆盖度低于0.2 rpm的转录本。将这些分配了第1个暂停区域的转录本用于进一步分析。在1小时生长素处理的NELF-C-AID细胞中，第1个暂停区域下游+10至+500 bp区域PRO-seq覆盖度最大的核苷酸位置被指定为第2个暂停区域。对于热休克实验，将基因体（第1个暂停区域+500 bp至+100 kb或转录终止位点）处PRO-seq覆盖度在热休克处理后增加或减少超过1.5倍的转录本分别指定为HS上调或HS下调基因。使用deepTools 3.0.0/3.1.1/3.1.2 computeMatrix、plotHeatmap和plotProfile（Ramı´rez等，2016）生成热图和Molecular Cell 78, 261–274.e1–e5, 2020年4月16日 e3的PRO-seq信号元图。使用deepTools 3.0.0/3.1.1/3.1.2 bigwigCompare计算核苷酸分辨率下的PRO-seq信号log2倍数变化，伪计数为0.25 rpm。

ChIP-seq Library Preparation｜ChIP-seq 文库制备#

ChIP-seq was performed as described previously (Liang et al., 2018) with minor modification. Briefly, 20-50 million cells were crosslinked with 1% paraformaldehyde (Electron Microscopy Sciences) in PBS for 10 min at r.t. and were quenched with 0.2 M glycine for 5 min. Spike-in mouse embryonic fibroblast fixed cells were added to samples as described previously (Orlando et al., 2014) for NELF-C, NELF-E, SPT5, Pol II CTD Ser2P, Pol II CTD Ser5P, XRN2, DCP2 ChIP-seq in NELF-C-AID cells. Chromatin was sonicated with the Covaris E220 for 4 min with 10% duty cycle, 140 peak intensity power, 200 cycles per burst. Immunoprecipitations were carried out overnight with antibodies as indicated (see antibodies section) and Protein A/G-agarose beads (Santa Cruz) or Dynabeads Protein G (Invitrogen). Protein degradation by 400 mg/ml Protease K (roche) and reverse crosslinking reaction were performed at 65� C overnight. Immunoprecipitated DNA was purified by chloroform extraction and ethanol precipitation or using QIAquick PCR purification kit (QIAGEN). DNA libraries were prepared by the HTP Library Preparation Kit for Illumina (KAPA) and sequenced on the NextSeq 500 or NovaSeq 6000 (Illumina).

ChIP-seq 实验按照之前描述的方法进行（Liang 等，2018），略有修改。简言之，将 20-50 百万细胞在 PBS 中用 1% 戊二醛（Electron Microscopy Sciences）于室温交联 10 分钟，随后用 0.2 M 甘油醛淬灭 5 分钟。按照之前描述的方法（Orlando 等，2014），向样品中加入 Spike-in 小鼠胚胎成纤维细胞固定细胞，用于 NELF-C、NELF-E、SPT5、RNA 聚合酶 II CTD Ser2P、RNA 聚合酶 II CTD Ser5P、XRN2、DCP2 的 ChIP-seq 实验，这些实验均在 NELF-C-AID 细胞中进行。染色质使用 Covaris E220 进行超声破碎 4 分钟，设置为 10% 占空比、140 峰值强度功率和每脉冲 200 次循环。免疫沉淀反应在 indicated 抗体（见抗体部分）存在下进行过夜，并使用 Protein A/G-琼脂糖 beads（Santa Cruz）或 Dynabeads Protein G（Invitrogen）。使用 400 mg/ml 蛋白酶 K（roche）进行蛋白降解，然后在 65°C 下进行逆转交联反应过夜。免疫沉淀的 DNA 使用氯仿抽提和乙醇沉淀或 QIAquick PCR 纯化试剂盒（QIAGEN）进行纯化。DNA 文库使用 Illumina 的 HTP 文库制备试剂盒（KAPA）制备，并在 NextSeq 500 或 NovaSeq 6000（Illumina）上进行测序。

ChIP-seq Alignment and Data Analysis｜ChIP-seq 比对与数据分析#

Single-end raw reads trimmed by Trimmomatic 0.33 (Bolger et al., 2014) were aligned to a concatenated genome comprised of human hg19 and mouse mm9 assemblies, using bowtie 2.2.6 (Langmead and Salzberg, 2012) with —sensitive option. The aligned reads with MAPQ R 30 were extended to 150 bp and read counts were normalized to total reads aligned to spike-in genome. Metaplots of ChIP-seq signal were generated using deepTools 3.0.0/3.1.1/3.1.2 computeMatrix, plotHeatmap, plotProfile (Ramı´rez et al., 2016). Peaks were called using MACS 2.1.0 (Zhang et al., 2008) with a q-value cutoff of 0.05. Venn diagrams were generated using intervene 0.6.4 (Khan and Mathelier, 2017) with bedtools 2.25.0. For the correlation plot in Figure 6B, reads at each pause site ± 2 kb were counted using featureCounts 2.0.0 (Liao et al., 2014) then read counts were visualized in R 3.3.3. Peak annotation for NELF-C and CBP80 ChIP-seq in Figure S6A was performed using HOMER 4.10 (Heinz et al., 2010). Merged heatmap in Figure S6B was prepared by stacking heatmaps with reduced opacity. MNase-seq Data Analysis｜MNase-seq 数据分析#

MNase-seq single-end raw reads in DLD-1 cells (Yamashita et al., 2011) (SRA: DRX000003) were used to determine nucleosome positions. Low quality bases were trimmed from the 3′ end and 16–36 bp reads were taken by Trimmomatic 0.33 (Bolger et al., 2014). Processed reads were aligned to the human hg19 genome with bowtie 1.1.2 (Langmead et al., 2009) with options -m 1 -v 2. The resulting BAM files were sorted and converted to BED files. The BED files were processed by improved nucleosome-positioning algorithm iNPS 1.2.2 (Chen et al., 2014) to determine nucleosome positions. For annotation of the +1 nucleosome, the closest nucleosome that was found downstream of the pause region for each gene and that was annotated as MainPeak by iNPS was chosen. Note that nucleosomes that did not overlap the regions of H3K4me3 ChIP-seq peaks ± 75 bp were excluded to discard falsepositive nucleosome positions. The resulting nucleosomes are referred to as the +1 nucleosome.

使用了 DLD-1 细胞中 MNase-seq 单端原始读段数据（Yamashita 等，2011；SRA: DRX000003）来确定核小体位置。通过 Trimmomatic 0.33（Bolger 等，2014）修剪 3’ 端的低质量碱基，并选取 16–36 bp 的读段。处理后的读段使用 bowtie 1.1.2（Langmead 等，2009）比对到人类 hg19 基因组，参数为 -m 1 -v 2。生成的 BAM 文件被排序并转换为 BED 文件。随后使用改进的核小体定位算法 iNPS 1.2.2（Chen 等，2014）处理 BED 文件以确定核小体位置。对于 +1 核小体的注释，选择每个基因暂停区域下游最近的一个核小体，并且该核小体被 iNPS 注释为 MainPeak。注意，未与 H3K4me3 ChIP-seq 峰 ± 75 bp 区域重叠的核小体被排除以避免假阳性核小体位置。所得到的核小体被称为 +1 核小体。

PRO-cap Library Preparation and Read Alignment｜PRO-cap 文库制备与读段比对#

PRO-cap libraries were prepared as described previously (Kwak et al., 2013; Mahat et al., 2016b) with minor modifications. NELF-CAID cells were treated with or without 500 mM auxin for 2 h, followed by nuclear extraction. �10 million NELF-C-AID nuclei with 0.5 million spike-in Drosophila S2 nuclei were subjected to nuclear run-on reaction. Uncapped RNA with 5′-monophosphate was degraded using Terminator 5′-phosphate dependent exonuclease (Lucigen). 5′ triphosphate and monophosphate from uncapped RNA were removed using Quick CIP (NEB). 5′ cap repair was performed with RppH (NEB). For adaptor ligation, PRO-seq adaptor sequences were used instead of PRO-cap adapters. Libraries were then sequenced by NextSeq 500 in paired-end sequencing mode. Raw reads were processed as described for PRO-seq above, requiring a read length of 18–36 bp. Processed paired-end reads were aligned to a concatenated genome comprised of hg19 and dm3 assemblies using bowtie2 (Langmead and Salzberg, 2012) with options (—very-sensitive -X 1000 —no-mixed —no-discordant —no-unal) as described previously (Tome et al., 2018). The 5′ ends of aligned R2 reads with MAPQ R 40 were taken by bedtools genomecov 2.25.0 (Quinlan and Hall, 2010) with -strand and �5 options. Read counts were normalized to total reads aligned to the spike-in genome.

PRO-cap 文库按照之前描述的方法制备（Kwak 等，2013；Mahat 等，2016b），并进行了小幅修改。NELF-CAID 细胞在有或无 500 mM 生长素处理 2 h 后进行核提取。将 1000 万个 NELF-C-AID 核以及 50 万个 spike-in 果蝇 S2 核用于核运行反应。使用 Terminator 5’ 磷酸依赖性外切酶（Lucigen）降解具有 5’ 单磷酸基团的未封帽 RNA。使用 Quick CIP（NEB）去除未封帽 RNA 中的 5’ 三磷酸和单磷酸。使用 RppH（NEB）进行 5’ 封帽修复。在接头连接步骤中，使用 PRO-seq 接头序列代替 PRO-cap 接头。随后使用 NextSeq 500 以双端测序模式对文库进行测序。原始读段的处理方法与上述 PRO-seq 相同，要求读段长度为 18–36 bp。处理后的双端读段使用 bowtie2（Langmead 和 Salzberg，2012）比对到由 hg19 和 dm3 组装构成的拼接基因组，参数为 (—very-sensitive -X 1000 —no-mixed —no-discordant —no-unal)，如之前所述（Tome 等，2018）。使用 bedtools genomecov 2.25.0（Quinlan 和 Hall，2010）提取比对到 R2 读段的 5’ 端，参数为 -strand 和 -5。读段计数根据比对到 spike-in 基因组的总读段数进行归一化。

PRO-cap Data Analysis｜PRO-cap 数据分析#

de novo transcription start sites were identified from PRO-cap data using HOMER 4.11 with findcsRNATSS option (Duttke et al., 2019; Heinz et al., 2010). TSS identified in untreated and auxin-treated samples were merged then defined as TSS regions. Raw PRO-cap reads were counted using HOMER 4.11 annotatePeaks. Read counts were normalized to spike-in counts using RUVseq with RUVg option (Risso et al., 2014) in R 3.3.3. Differential expression analysis was performed using DESeq2 (Love et al., 2014) and visualized in R 3.3.3. TSS with significant gain of PRO-cap signal upon NELF loss (Benjamini-Hochberg adjusted p value < 0.05) was e4 Molecular Cell 78, 261–274.e1–e5, April 16, 2020 defined as cap-up TSS, while TSS with significant loss of PRO-cap signal upon NELF loss (adjusted p value < 0.05) was defined as cap-down TSS. Metaplots of PRO-cap and PRO-seq at cap-up TSS or cap-down TSS were generated using deepTools 3.1.1. For heatmaps of ChIP-seq and MNase-seq in Figure 7D, TSS were merged with gap of no longer than 600 bp to obtain TSS clusters. Heatmaps were then generated at cap-up TSS clusters and cap-down TSS clusters. To measure distances of PRO-cap signals between untreated and 2h auxin-treated NELF-C-AID cells, each position of TSS in the untreated sample was compared with the position of the nearest TSS in the treated sample in a strand-specific manner using HOMER 4.11 annotatePeaks.

使用 HOMER 4.11 的 findcsRNATSS 选项，从 PRO-cap 数据中鉴定出从头转录起始位点（de novo transcription start sites）（Duttke 等，2019；Heinz 等，2010）。在未处理和生长素处理样本中鉴定出的转录起始位点（TSS）被合并，然后定义为 TSS 区域。使用 HOMER 4.11 的 annotatePeaks 对原始 PRO-cap 读段进行计数。使用 RUVseq 并结合 RUVg 选项（Risso 等，2014），在 R 3.3.3 中将读段计数标准化至 spike-in 计数。使用 DESeq2（Love 等，2014）进行差异表达分析，并在 R 3.3.3 中进行可视化。在 NELF 缺失时 PRO-cap 信号显著增加的 TSS（Benjamini-Hochberg 校正 p 值 < 0.05）被定义为 cap-up TSS，而在 NELF 缺失时 PRO-cap 信号显著减少的 TSS（校正 p 值 < 0.05）被定义为 cap-down TSS。使用 deepTools 3.1.1 生成 cap-up TSS 或 cap-down TSS 的 PRO-cap 和 PRO-seq 的元图谱（metaplots）。对于图 7D 中 ChIP-seq 和 MNase-seq 的热图，将 TSS 以不超过 600 bp 的间隔合并以获得 TSS 簇。随后在 cap-up TSS 簇和 cap-down TSS 簇中生成热图。为了测量未处理和 2 h 生长素处理的 NELF-C-AID 细胞之间 PRO-cap 信号的距离，使用 HOMER 4.11 的 annotatePeaks 以链特异性方式将未处理样本中的每个 TSS 位置与处理样本中最近的 TSS 位置进行比较。

提示#

该研究通过快速降解NELF复合物，发现RNA聚合酶II在启动子附近存在两步暂停现象，且第一到第二步暂停的转换不依赖于P-TEFb活性。同时，NELF缺失不影响热休克反应，但影响帽结合复合物的招募和mRNA帽结构稳定性。
