SPT5 stabilization of promoter-proximal RNA polymerase II

SPT5 对启动子附近 RNA Pol II 的稳定作用

原论文

Highlights ｜要点#

  • Acute depletion of SPT5 triggers degradation of the RNA Pol II subunit RPB1.

    急性耗竭 SPT5 会触发 RNA Pol II 亚基 RPB1 降解。

  • CUL3 is recruited to RNA Pol II following SPT5 loss and contributes to RPB1 degradation.

    SPT5 缺失后，CUL3 被招募至 RNA Pol II，并参与 RPB1 降解。

  • VCP inhibition separates SPT5’s roles in RNA Pol II stability and elongation.

    抑制 VCP 有助于区分 SPT5 对 RNA Pol II 稳定性和延伸的作用。

  • SPT5 stabilizes promoter-proximal RNA Pol II and permits its release into gene bodies.

    SPT5 稳定启动子附近的 RNA Pol II，使其得以释放并进入基因体。

Summary ｜摘要#

Based on in vitro studies, it has been demonstrated that the DSIF complex, composed of SPT4 and SPT5, regulates the elongation stage of transcription catalyzed by RNA polymerase II (RNA Pol II). The precise cellular function of SPT5 is not clear, because conventional gene depletion strategies for SPT5 result in loss of cellular viability. Using an acute inducible protein depletion strategy to circumvent this issue, we report that SPT5 loss triggers the ubiquitination and proteasomal degradation of the core RNA Pol II subunit RPB1, a process that we show to be evolutionarily conserved from yeast to human cells. RPB1 degradation requires the E3 ligase Cullin 3, the VCP/p97 unfoldase, and a novel form of CDK9 kinase complex. Our study demonstrates that SPT5 stabilizes RNA Pol II specifically at promoter-proximal regions, permitting RNA Pol II release from promoters into gene bodies and providing mechanistic insight into the cellular function of SPT5 in safeguarding accurate gene expression.

体外研究已证实，由 SPT4 和 SPT5 组成的 DSIF 复合物调控 RNA 聚合酶 II（RNA Pol II）催化的转录延伸阶段。由于传统的 SPT5 基因耗竭策略会导致细胞死亡，SPT5 在细胞内的确切功能尚不清楚。为规避这一问题，我们采用急性诱导蛋白耗竭策略，发现 SPT5 缺失会触发 RNA Pol II 核心亚基 RPB1 的泛素化及蛋白酶体降解，并证明这一过程从酵母到人类细胞具有进化保守性。RPB1 降解需要 E3 连接酶 Cullin 3、解折叠酶 VCP/p97，以及一种新型 CDK9 激酶复合物。本研究表明，SPT5 特异性地稳定启动子附近区域的 RNA Pol II，使其得以从启动子释放并进入基因体，从机制上阐明了 SPT5 在细胞内保障准确基因表达的功能。

Introduction ｜引言#

Transcriptional regulation by RNA polymerase II (RNA Pol II) in eukaryotic cells is a multi-step process regulated by the function of numerous transcription factors safeguarding accurate developmental gene expression (Chen et al., 2018). One such regulated step in gene expression is RNA Pol II elongation control, which includes promoter-proximal pause release and productive elongation (Chen et al., 2018). Promoter-proximal pausing is a specific regulatory step that immediately follows initiation before productive elongating by RNA Pol II. This step has been shown to be highly regulated, and its misregulation is associated with diverse human diseases (Brown et al., 1996; Chen et al., 2018). For RNA Pol II to efficiently transcribe mRNA during the promoter-proximal pause/release and productive elongation stages of transcription, it must overcome physical obstacles such as nucleosomes, be able to be a platform for proper splicing, and deal with many other transcriptional barriers and impediments (Brown et al., 1996). In so doing, elongating RNA Pol II associates with numerous factors to safeguard accurate gene expression around such obstacles. However, how cells determine and build properly equipped RNA Pol II to transverse into the productive elongation stage from the promoter-proximal regions remains largely unknown.

真核细胞中，RNA 聚合酶 II（RNA Pol II）的转录调控涉及多个步骤，众多转录因子共同参与，以保障发育过程中准确的基因表达（Chen 等，2018）。其中一个受调控的步骤是 RNA Pol II 的延伸控制，包括启动子附近暂停释放和有效延伸（Chen 等，2018）。启动子附近暂停是一种特定的调控步骤，发生于转录起始之后、RNA Pol II 进入有效延伸之前。这一步骤受到严格调控，其失调与多种人类疾病有关（Brown 等，1996；Chen 等，2018）。为在启动子附近暂停及暂停释放和有效延伸阶段高效转录 mRNA，RNA Pol II 必须克服核小体等物理障碍，为正确剪接提供平台，并应对其他多种转录屏障（Brown 等，1996）。在此过程中，延伸中的 RNA Pol II 与众多因子结合，以在遇到这些障碍时仍能保障准确的基因表达。然而，细胞如何识别并组装配备适当因子的 RNA Pol II，使其从启动子附近区域进入有效延伸阶段，目前仍不清楚。

SPT5 is an evolutionarily conserved and essential elongation factor (Decker, 2021). In vitro studies demonstrate that SPT5 forms a heterodimer with SPT4, called DSIF, and it is required for pausing and productive elongation (Wada et al., 1998). In vitro studies show that a point mutation of SPT5 can lead to failure in pausing, whereas its elongation activity remains as effective as wild type (Guo et al., 2000), suggesting that SPT5’s roles in pausing and elongation are mechanistically distinct from one other. In vitro transcriptional studies also demonstrate that RNA Pol II pausing requires the NELF complex, together with DSIF, by forming the RNA Pol II-DSIF-NELF pausing complex (Yamaguchi et al., 1999, 2002). The structure of this complex has suggested that NELF may restrain RNA Pol II mobility, allowing RNA Pol II to stay in the pausing state (Vos et al., 2018).

SPT5 是一个进化上保守且必需的延伸因子（Decker，2021）。体外研究表明，SPT5 与 SPT4 形成异二聚体，称为 DSIF，并且对于暂停和有效延伸是必需的（Wada 等，1998）。体外研究表明，SPT5 的一个点突变会导致暂停失败，而其延伸活性与野生型一样有效（Guo 等，2000），这表明 SPT5 在暂停和延伸中的作用机制上是不同的。体外转录研究还表明，RNA Pol II 暂停需要 NELF 复合物，与 DSIF 共同形成 RNA Pol II-DSIF-NELF 暂停复合物（Yamaguchi 等，1999，2002）。该复合物的结构表明，NELF 可能限制 RNA Pol II 的移动性，使 RNA Pol II 能够保持在暂停状态（Vos 等，2018）。

Recently, we demonstrated that promoter-proximal RNA Pol II is still paused at the +1 nucleosome-associated regions upon acute depletion of NELF in vivo (Aoi et al., 2020). This suggests that RNA Pol II undergoes 2-step pausing at promoters: pausing with NELF and pausing at the +1 nucleosome. Supporting this model, depletion of histone variant H2A.Z.1, which is enriched at the +1 nucleosome, has been shown to increase the release of RNA Pol II into gene bodies (Mylonas et al., 2021). In contrast, it has been reported that SPT6 depletion results in the accumulation of RNA Pol II at the +1 nucleosome (Zumer et al., 2021). These acute depletion studies suggest that in addition to NELF dissociation, there appears to be a regulatory mechanism to promote RNA Pol II release through the +1 nucleosome into gene bodies. These studies also imply that solely looking at in vitro transcriptional studies in test tubes may not faithfully recapitulate the in vivo function of such factors and their roles in transcriptional control in living cells. Indeed, how SPT5 regulates pausing at the +1 nucleosome remains poorly understood.

我们最近证明，在体内急性耗竭 NELF 后，启动子附近的 RNA Pol II 仍会在 +1 核小体相关区域暂停（Aoi 等，2020）。这提示 RNA Pol II 在启动子处经历两步暂停：先是与 NELF 结合时的暂停，随后是在 +1 核小体处的暂停。支持这一模型的证据是，耗竭富集于 +1 核小体的组蛋白变体 H2A.Z.1，可促进 RNA Pol II 释放并进入基因体（Mylonas 等，2021）。相反，有研究报道，耗竭 SPT6 会使 RNA Pol II 在 +1 核小体处积累（Zumer 等，2021）。这些急性耗竭研究提示，除 NELF 解离外，似乎还存在促进 RNA Pol II 穿过 +1 核小体并释放至基因体的调控机制。这些研究也提示，单纯依赖试管中的体外转录研究，可能无法忠实重现这些因子在体内的功能及其在活细胞转录控制中的作用。事实上，SPT5 如何调控 +1 核小体处的暂停，仍缺乏清晰认识。

Genome-wide studies demonstrate that SPT5 interacts with RNA Pol II throughout the elongation phase (Pavri et al., 2010; Rahl et al., 2010), and it has been proposed that phosphorylation of SPT5 by a positive elongation factor P-TEFb stimulates SPT5’s elongation function (Yamada et al., 2006). In vivo, P-TEFb predominantly phosphorylates SPT5 at two distinct loci: the C-terminal repeat (CTR) domain and the Kyprides, Ouzounis, Woese (KOW) 4-KOW5 linker region (Sansó et al., 2016). Although CTR phosphorylation promotes productive elongation in vitro (Yamada et al., 2006) and in vivo (Cortazar et al., 2019; Parua et al., 2018), CTR is dispensable for cell viability (Komori et al., 2009), suggesting that CTR phosphorylation might not be required as a core function of SPT5. The Integrator complex coupled with PP2A phosphatase has been shown to remove phosphorylation at the KOW4-KOW5 linker, facilitating premature termination at promoters (Huang et al., 2020).

全基因组研究表明，SPT5 在延伸阶段与 RNA Pol II 相互作用（Pavri 等，2010；Rahl 等，2010），并且有研究提出，正性转录延伸因子 P-TEFb 对 SPT5 的磷酸化可增强其延伸功能（Yamada 等，2006）。在体内，P-TEFb 主要在两个不同位点磷酸化 SPT5：C 端重复（CTR）结构域和 Kyprides, Ouzounis, Woese（KOW）4-KOW5 连接区（Sansó 等，2016）。尽管 CTR 磷酸化在体外（Yamada 等，2006）和体内（Cortazar 等，2019；Parua 等，2018）均促进有效延伸，但 CTR 对于细胞存活并非必需（Komori 等，2009），这表明 CTR 磷酸化可能不是 SPT5 的核心功能。与 PP2A 磷酸酶偶联的 Integrator 复合物已被证明能够去除 KOW4-KOW5 连接区的磷酸化，从而促进启动子处的提前终止（Huang 等，2020）。

Although SPT5 has been shown to possess important roles in transcriptional regulation in vitro, previous studies looking at SPT5’s in vivo function either via RNAi or by gene deletion methods were limited by SPT5 being an essential gene, with its depletion resulting in cell death (Fitz et al., 2018; Rahl et al., 2010). Here, we use an acute and rapid depletion system for SPT5 loss in human cells to dissect the in vivo roles in transcription by RNA Pol II. We find a previously unrecognized function for SPT5 and a mechanism of action in regulating RNA Pol II stability to facilitate release of RNA Pol II from promoter-proximal regions to gene bodies.

尽管 SPT5 在体外已被证明在转录调控中具有重要作用，但以往通过 RNAi 或基因缺失方法研究 SPT5 体内功能的研究受到 SPT5 作为必需基因的限制，其耗竭会导致细胞死亡（Fitz 等，2018；Rahl 等，2010）。在此，我们利用人类细胞中 SPT5 急性且快速缺失系统来解析 RNA Pol II 转录中的体内作用。我们发现 SPT5 的一种此前未被认识的功能及其调控 RNA Pol II 稳定性的机制，从而促进 RNA Pol II 从启动子附近区域释放到基因体。

To precisely investigate SPT5 function in living human cells, we generated an auxin-inducible degron (AID) (Natsume et al., 2016) knockin DLD-1 cell line in which the AID-tagged SPT5 is expressed from its endogenous genomic locus. This cell line allowed the acute depletion of SPT5-AID within ~2 h of auxin treatment (Figures 1A and 1B) while avoiding secondary effects of its long-term depletion, including severe growth defect and cell death (Figure S1A). As a control, the untagged SPT5 in parental cells was not degraded by auxin treatment, confirming the AID-dependent degradation in SPT5-AID cells. Following SPT5 depletion, SPT4 levels were somewhat decreased (Figure 1B). This indicates that SPT4 stability depends on SPT5, likely through the formation of the SPT4-SPT5 heterodimer complex DSIF. Unexpectedly, we also observed an approximately 80% reduction of RPB1, the largest subunit of RNA Pol II, following a decrease in the SPT5 levels (Figure 1B). Other RNA Pol II subunits, RPB3 and RPB5, seem to be more stable upon SPT5 loss (Figures S1B and S1C). Although RPB1 and RPB3 are RNA Pol II-specific subunits, RPB5 is a common subunit of RNA polymerase I (RNA Pol I), RNA Pol II, and RNA polymerase III (RNA Pol III). This suggests that the stability of RPB5 in the absence of SPT5 may be the result of its incorporation in RNA Pol I and RNA Pol III.

为了精确研究 SPT5 在活的人类细胞中的功能，我们生成了一个生长素诱导降解系统（AID）敲入的 DLD-1 细胞系，在该细胞系中，AID 标记的 SPT5 从其内源性基因组位点表达。该细胞系允许在生长素处理 2 h 后急性耗竭 SPT5-AID（图 1A 和 1B），同时避免了长期耗竭所导致的二次效应，包括严重的生长缺陷和细胞死亡（图 S1A）。作为对照，亲本细胞中的未标记 SPT5 不会被生长素处理降解，这证实了 SPT5-AID 细胞中依赖于 AID 的降解。在 SPT5 耗竭后，SPT4 水平有所下降（图 1B）。这表明 SPT4 的稳定性依赖于 SPT5，可能是通过形成 SPT4-SPT5 异二聚体复合物 DSIF 实现的。令人意外的是，我们还观察到在 SPT5 水平下降后，RNA Pol II 最大亚基 RPB1 大约减少了 80%（图 1B）。其他 RNA Pol II 亚基，如 RPB3 和 RPB5，在 SPT5 缺失时似乎更加稳定（图 S1B 和 S1C）。尽管 RPB1 和 RPB3 是 RNA Pol II 特异性亚基，但 RPB5 是 RNA 聚合酶 I（RNA Pol I）、RNA 聚合酶 II（RNA Pol II）和 RNA 聚合酶 III（RNA Pol III）的共同亚基。这表明在缺乏 SPT5 的情况下，RPB5 的稳定性可能是由于其被整合进 RNA Pol I 和 RNA Pol III 所致。

To determine whether RPB1 loss is a general phenomenon of elongation factor depletion or specific to SPT5, we next treated NELF-C-AID, NELF-E-AID (Aoi et al., 2020), PAF1-AID (Chen et al., 2017), and SPT4-AID cells with auxin and tested for RNA Pol II stability within the same time frame as SPT5. Depletion of PAF1, NELF, or SPT4 did not affect RPB1 levels (Figures 1C, 1D, S1D, and S1E), strongly suggesting that regulation of RPB1 stability is a SPT5-specific function and not a general function associated with elongation factors depletion. SPT5 levels are slightly decreased upon SPT4-AID degradation, but a significant amount of SPT5 remained compared with depletion levels of SPT5-AID (Figure S1E). It is likely that the remaining amount of SPT5 is sufficient to maintain RPB1 stability in the absence of SPT4. We also demonstrated that inhibition of protein synthesis still resulted in the observed RPB1 loss (Figure 1E), revealing that SPT5 depletion results in RPB1 degradation.

为了确定 RPB1 的丢失是否是延伸因子耗竭的一般现象，还是特异性于 SPT5，我们接下来对 NELF-C-AID、NELF-E-AID（Aoi et al., 2020）、PAF1-AID（Chen et al., 2017）和 SPT4-AID 细胞进行生长素处理，并在与 SPT5 相同的时间范围内检测 RNA Pol II 的稳定性。耗竭 PAF1、NELF 或 SPT4 并未影响 RPB1 水平（图 1C，1D，S1D 和 S1E），强烈表明对 RPB1 稳定性的调控是 SPT5 特有的功能，而不是与延伸因子耗竭相关的普遍功能。在 SPT4-AID 降解后，SPT5 水平略有下降，但与 SPT5-AID 耗竭水平相比，仍保留了相当数量的 SPT5（图 S1E）。这可能表明剩余的 SPT5 量足以维持在缺乏 SPT4 情况下的 RPB1 稳定性。我们还证明，在抑制蛋白质合成的情况下，仍然观察到 RPB1 的丢失（图 1E），揭示了 SPT5 耗竭导致 RPB1 降解。

To further examine the degradation dynamics of RPB1, we prepared nucleoplasmic and chromatin fractions from auxintreated SPT5-AID cells. As observed in whole-cell extracts, RPB1 levels were decreased in both nucleoplasmic and chromatin fractions (Figure S2A). To test whether SPT5 depletion affects the phosphorylation status of RPB1, we blotted for phosphoserine 2 (S2P) and phosphoserine 5 (S5P) of the C-terminal domain (CTD) of RPB1. S5P levels were less stable than S2P levels (Figure S2A), suggesting that RPB1-S5P may be preferentially targeted for degradation upon SPT5 depletion.

为了进一步研究 RPB1 的降解动力学，我们从生长素处理的 SPT5-AID 细胞中制备了核质和染色质组分。正如在全细胞提取物中观察到的那样，RPB1 水平在核质和染色质组分中均降低（图 S2A）。为了检测 SPT5 耗竭是否影响 RPB1 的磷酸化状态，我们对 RPB1 C 端结构域（CTD）的磷酸丝氨酸 2（S2P）和磷酸丝氨酸 5（S5P）进行了检测。S5P 水平比 S2P 水平更不稳定（图 S2A），这表明在 SPT5 耗竭时，RPB1-S5P 可能优先被靶向降解。

SPT5 physically interacts with RNA Pol II (Wada et al., 1998) and co-localizes with RNA Pol II at most transcriptionally active genes on chromatin (Pavri et al., 2010; Rahl et al., 2010), suggesting that chromatin-bound RNA Pol II complexes mostly exist with SPT5. We reasoned that SPT5 depletion may affect the protein-protein interaction properties/composition of RNA Pol II transcription units, which can trigger the RPB1 degradation pathway. To examine relative amounts of RNA Pol II interacting proteins, we immunoprecipitated the endogenous RNA Pol II complex from the nuclease-digested chromatin fraction and identified co-immunoprecipitated proteins by mass spectrometry in both the presence and the absence of SPT5 (Figures S2B–S2D; Table S1). We normalized protein levels to RNA Pol II abundance in each condition, because RPB1 is partially degraded in the chromatin fraction after auxin treatment for 2 h (Figures S2A and S2D). As expected, SPT5 and SPT4 levels were significantly decreased upon auxin treatment in these purifications (Figure 2A), which validates our mass spectrometric quantification method. We observed a reduction of elongation factor ELOF1 (Figure 2A), which is consistent with its known interaction with SPT5 and RNA Pol II (Ehara et al., 2019). Cap methyltransferase CMTR1 was also reduced, suggesting a possible role for SPT5 in CMTR1 recruitment similar to the recruitment of capping enzyme by SPT5 (Doamekpor et al., 2014). We observed that levels of other RNA Pol II-associated complexes were largely unchanged (Figure 2B).

Because the proteasome can be recruited by the SPT5-AID degradation process, we focused on CUL3’s and VCP’s possible roles in RPB1 degradation.

Indeed, AID degradation requires the E3 ligase CUL1 (Natsume et al., 2016), but not CUL3. It has been shown that DNA damage induces RPB1 degradation, which requires E3 ubiquitin ligase NEDD4 (Anindya et al., 2007) or CUL5 (Yasukawa et al., 2008). Unexpectedly, knockdown of NEDD4 or CUL5 did not stabilize RPB1 upon SPT5 loss (Figures 3A–3C and S3B). 已有研究表明，DNA 损伤可诱导 RPB1 的降解，这一过程需要 E3 泛素连接酶 NEDD4（Anindya 等，2007）或 CUL5（Yasukawa 等，2008）。令人意外的是，在 SPT5 丢失时，敲低 NEDD4 或 CUL5 并未稳定 RPB1（图 3A–3C 和 S3B）。总体而言，我们的结果表明，在没有 SPT5 的情况下，需要 CUL3 进行 RPB1 的降解，而 NEDD4 或 CUL5 则不需要。

To further examine CUL3’s role in RPB1 degradation, we performed ChIP-seq for CUL3. In the untreated condition, the CUL3 signal was barely detectable on chromatin via ChIP (Figures 3D and S3C). This ChIP-seq result is consistent with our proteomics data, in which the amount of RNA Pol II-associated CUL3 was increased upon SPT5-AID degradation. We validated the CUL3 ChIP-seq signal by CUL3 knockdown experiments (Figures S3C and S3D). Altogether, our data strongly suggest CUL3-RNA Pol II association on chromatin that is triggered by SPT5-AID degradation.

VCP is known to mediate the unfolding of ubiquitylated proteins through its ATPase activity, leading to efficient protein degradation (van den Boom and Meyer, 2018). To test whether VCP is required for RPB1 degradation upon SPT5 loss, we performed shRNA against VCP. VCP depletion by 2 independent shRNA constructs appeared to partially stabilize RPB1 in the absence of SPT5 (Figure 4A). To confirm this result, we treated SPT5-AID cells with NMS-873 (Magnaghi et al., 2013), a potent inhibitor of VCP ATPase activity. The pharmacological inhibition of VCP significantly stabilized RPB1 upon SPT5 loss (Figures 4B and S4A), indicating that VCP ATPase activity is required for RPB1 degradation in the absence of SPT5.

已知 VCP 通过其 ATP 酶活性介导泛素化蛋白的解折叠，从而导致高效的蛋白质降解（van den Boom 和 Meyer，2018）。为了检测 VCP 是否对于 SPT5 缺失时的 RPB1 降解是必需的，我们进行了针对 VCP 的 shRNA 实验。通过两种独立的 shRNA 构建体进行的 VCP 耗竭似乎在没有 SPT5 的情况下部分稳定了 RPB1（图 4A）。为确认这一结果，我们用 NMS-873（Magnaghi 等，2013）处理 SPT5-AID 细胞，NMS-873 是一种强效的 VCP ATP 酶活性抑制剂。对 VCP 的药物抑制显著稳定了在 SPT5 缺失时的 RPB1（图 4B 和 S4A），表明在没有 SPT5 的情况下，VCP ATP 酶活性对于 RPB1 降解是必需的。

It has been shown that a defect in RNA Pol II initiation causes RPB1 degradation (Titov et al., 2011; Vispé et al., 2009). SPT5 is known to regulate elongation (Wada et al., 1998), and a recent report has suggested that SPT5 also regulates initiation (Diamant et al., 2016). This prompted us to test whether an initiation defect induces VCP-mediated RPB1 degradation. Unexpectedly, VCP inhibition did not stabilize RPB1 when inhibiting initiation by triptolide (TPL) (Figure 4C). This result revealed that there are at least two distinct RPB1 degradation pathways: (1) VCP-dependent degradation induced by SPT5 depletion and (2) VCP-independent degradation induced by a TPL-stimulated initiation defect. By dissecting these two degradation pathways, we concluded that in the absence of SPT5, RPB1 is degraded either during the early elongation stage at promoter-proximal regions or during the productive elongation stage in gene bodies, but not during the initiation stage.

已有研究表明，RNA Pol II 起始缺陷会导致 RPB1 降解（Titov 等，2011；Vispé 等，2009）。SPT5 已知可调节延伸（Wada 等，1998），最近的一项报告表明 SPT5 也可调节起始（Diamant 等，2016）。这促使我们测试起始缺陷是否诱导 VCP 介导的 RPB1 降解。出乎意料的是，在使用雷公藤甲素（TPL）抑制起始时，VCP 抑制并未稳定 RPB1（图 4C）。这一结果揭示了至少存在两种不同的 RPB1 降解途径：（1）由 SPT5 耗竭诱导的 VCP 依赖性降解；（2）由 TPL 刺激的起始缺陷诱导的 VCP 非依赖性降解。通过剖析这两种降解途径，我们得出结论：在缺乏 SPT5 的情况下，RPB1 要么在启动子附近区域的早期延伸阶段被降解，要么在基因体中的有效延伸阶段被降解，但不会在起始阶段被降解。

SPT5 and VCP are evolutionarily conserved proteins among eukaryotes (van den Boom and Meyer, 2018; Wada et al., 1998). To examine whether the SPT5-VCP pathway for RPB1 degradation is conserved in other organisms, we generated Saccharomyces cerevisiae strains in which SPT5, or a combination of Cdc48 (a yeast ortholog of VCP) and SPT5, can be rapidly depleted from nuclei using the anchor-away technique (Haruki et al., 2008). Surprisingly, SPT5 depletion led to RPB1 loss, and Cdc48 codepletion suppressed this RPB1 loss (Figure S4B). Collectively, our experiments suggest an evolutionarily conserved function of SPT5 in stabilizing RNA Pol II.

SPT5 和 VCP 是真核生物中进化保守的蛋白质（van den Boom 和 Meyer，2018；Wada 等，1998）。为了检测 SPT5-VCP 途径在其他生物中是否同样参与 RPB1 降解，我们利用锚定远离技术（anchor-away technique）生成了酿酒酵母菌株，其中 SPT5 或 Cdc48（VCP 的酵母同源物）与 SPT5 的组合可从细胞核中快速耗竭（Haruki 等，2008）。令人惊讶的是，SPT5 耗竭导致 RPB1 丢失，而 Cdc48 共耗竭则抑制了这种 RPB1 丢失（图 S4B）。综合而言，我们的实验表明 SPT5 在稳定 RNA Pol II 方面具有进化保守的功能。

To determine how SPT5 depletion and VCP inhibition affect the steady state of transcription by RNA Pol II, we treated SPT5-AID cells with the VCP inhibitor, followed by auxin, and then performed ChIP-seq for RNA Pol II. In the absence of SPT5, peaks of RNA Pol II were barely detected compared with the vehicle control (Figures 5A, 5B, and S5A), likely because of RPB1 degradation. We asked whether the loss of the RNA Pol II signal as a result of SPT5 depletion could be suppressed by VCP inhibition. As shown (Figures 5A, 5B, and S5A), VCP inhibition restored RNA Pol II levels on chromatin in the absence of SPT5. ChIP-seq for SPT5 showed that the SPT5 signal was barely detectable upon SPT5 depletion even when inhibiting VCP (Figures 5A and 5B), which confirmed nearly complete depletion of SPT5 from chromatin. These SPT5 depletion experiments coupled with VCP inhibition allowed us to directly investigate how SPT5 regulates RNA Pol II transcription on chromatin while avoiding RNA Pol II loss due to RPB1 degradation. Careful analysis of the ChIP-seq data suggested that RNA Pol II levels were strongly accumulated at the 5′ end of gene bodies in the absence of SPT5 and in the presence of VCP inhibitors (Figures 5A, 5B, and S5A). In contrast, RNA Pol II at the rest of the gene bodies appeared to show a normal distribution pattern (Figures 5A, and S5A). This indicates that RPB1 degradation induced by SPT5 loss occurs during the early stage of elongation.

为了确定 SPT5 耗竭和 VCP 抑制如何影响 RNA Pol II 的稳态转录，我们对 SPT5-AID 细胞进行了 VCP 抑制剂处理，随后进行生长素处理，并对 RNA Pol II 进行染色质免疫沉淀测序（ChIP-seq）。在缺乏 SPT5 的情况下，与溶剂对照相比，RNA Pol II 峰几乎检测不到（图 5A、5B 和 S5A），这可能是由于 RPB1 降解所致。我们进一步检验，抑制 VCP 能否减轻 SPT5 耗竭所导致的 RNA Pol II 信号丢失。如图所示（图 5A、5B 和 S5A），在缺乏 SPT5 的情况下，VCP 抑制恢复了染色质上的 RNA Pol II 水平。对 SPT5 的 ChIP-seq 显示，即使在抑制 VCP 的情况下，SPT5 耗竭后 SPT5 信号几乎检测不到（图 5A 和 5B），这证实了 染色质上的 SPT5 已几乎完全耗竭。这些 SPT5 耗竭实验结合 VCP 抑制使我们能够直接研究 SPT5 如何调节染色质上的 RNA Pol II 转录，同时避免由于 RPB1 降解导致的 RNA Pol II 丢失。仔细分析 ChIP-seq 数据表明，在缺乏 SPT5 且存在 VCP 抑制剂的情况下，基因体 5′ 端 RNA Pol II 水平显著积累（图 5A、5B 和 S5A）。相比之下，基因体其余部分的 RNA Pol II 似乎表现出正常的分布模式（图 5A 和 S5A）。这表明 SPT5 缺失后诱导的 RPB1 降解发生在延伸早期。

We also observed RPB1 loss during early elongation in yeast, and codepletion of Cdc48 rescued this RPB1 loss (Figures S5B and S5C), suggesting an evolutionarily conserved mechanism of RNA Pol II stabilization by SPT5 during early elongation.

我们在酵母中也观察到 RPB1 在早期延伸阶段的缺失，并且共同耗竭 Cdc48 可以挽救这种 RPB1 的缺失（图 S5B 和 S5C），这表明 SPT5 在早期延伸阶段稳定 RNA Pol II 的机制具有进化保守性。

SPT5 loss specifically results in the loss of stability of promoter-proximal RNA Pol II, but not RNA Pol II in the productive elongation stage ｜ SPT5 的缺失特异性地导致启动子附近 RNA Pol II 不稳定，但不影响有效延伸阶段的 RNA Pol II#

To gain further insight into how SPT5 depletion and VCP inhibition affect the dynamics of RNA Pol II transcription during different stages of elongation, we performed a pulse-chase nascent RNA labeling assay to determine the fate of both early- (promoter-proximal) and late- (productive elongation) elongating RNA Pol II. In this assay, we treated SPT5-AID cells with P-TEFb inhibitor NVP-2 for 1 h, followed by in vivo nascent RNA labeling with 4-thiouridine (4sU). The P-TEFb inhibition blocks pause escape but does not alter the elongation form of RNA Pol II that has already escaped (Jonkers et al., 2014), allowing us to infer a distance that RNA Pol II travels during P-TEFb inhibition in the presence or absence of SPT5. By transient transcriptome sequencing (TT-seq) (Schwalb et al., 2016), we mapped genome-wide localization of 4sU-labeled nascent RNA. Surprisingly, RNA Pol II was able to travel substantial distances when SPT5 was depleted, although this distance is shorter than that of the untreated control (Figures 5C and 5D). This suggests that RNA Pol II at the late elongation stage is unlikely to be degraded in the absence of SPT5. We then estimated the elongation velocity in a time course treatment of NVP-2 (0, 30, and 60 min) (Figure S5D) at the single-gene level (Figure 5E) and meta-gene level (Figure S5E). Although the RNA Pol II velocity in the untreated condition is consistent with previously reported velocities for RNA Pol II (Jonkers et al., 2014), the velocity of SPT5-depleted RNA Pol II was significantly slower (Figures 5E and S5E). Stabilization of RNA Pol II by VCP inhibition did not rescue this slower velocity (Figures 5E and S5E). This indicates that even when RNA Pol II is stabilized at promoter-proximal regions, SPT5 is required for proper elongation velocity during the late elongation stage on the body of the genes. Collectively, our steady-state and pulse-chase analyses for RNA Pol II, together with dissection of degradation pathways, demonstrated that VCP-dependent RPB1 degradation upon SPT5 loss occurs not during the initiation stage or the fully productive elongation stage but rather during the early promoter-proximal stage. This conclusion is consistent with our finding that S5P, which is associated with promoter-proximal RNA Pol II, may be a target of degradation (Figure S2A).

为了进一步了解 SPT5 耗竭和 VCP 抑制如何影响 RNA Pol II 转录在延伸不同阶段的动态变化，我们进行了脉冲-追踪新生 RNA 标记实验，以确定早期（启动子附近）和晚期（有效延伸）延伸中的 RNA Pol II 的命运。在此实验中，我们用 P-TEFb 抑制剂 NVP-2 处理 SPT5-AID 细胞 1 小时，随后在体内使用 4-硫尿苷（4sU）标记新生 RNA。P-TEFb 抑制阻断了暂停释放过程，但不会改变已经逃逸的 RNA Pol II 的延伸形式（Jonkers 等，2014），使我们能够推断在存在或不存在 SPT5 的情况下，RNA Pol II 在 P-TEFb 抑制期间所行进的距离。通过瞬时转录组测序（TT-seq）（Schwalb 等，2016），我们绘制了 4sU 标记的新生 RNA 的全基因组定位。令人惊讶的是，在 SPT5 耗竭的情况下，RNA Pol II 仍能行进相当大的距离，尽管这一距离比未处理对照组要短（图 5C 和 5D）。这表明在没有 SPT5 的情况下，处于晚期延伸阶段的 RNA Pol II 不太可能被降解。随后，我们通过 NVP-2 的时间进程处理（0、30 和 60 分钟）（图 S5D），在单基因水平（图 5E）和汇总基因水平（图 S5E）估算延伸速度。尽管未处理条件下的 RNA Pol II 速度与之前报道的 RNA Pol II 速度一致（Jonkers 等，2014），但 SPT5 耗竭的 RNA Pol II 速度显著较慢（图 5E 和 S5E）。通过 VCP 抑制稳定 RNA Pol II 并不能挽救这种较慢的速度（图 5E 和 S5E）。这表明即使在 RNA Pol II 在启动子附近区域被稳定的情况下，SPT5 对于基因体上晚期延伸阶段的正常延伸速度也是必需的。综合我们的稳态和脉冲-追踪分析以及对降解途径的剖析，我们证明了在 SPT5 缺失时，VCP 依赖的 RPB1 降解并非发生在起始阶段或完全有效的延伸阶段，而是在早期启动子附近阶段发生。这一结论与我们发现 S5P（与启动子附近 RNA Pol II 相关）可能是降解靶标一致（图 S2A）。

Acute depletion of SPT5 leads to a pausing transition ｜急性耗竭 SPT5 导致暂停状态转变#

We recently demonstrated that NELF functions in vivo by regulating the transition of pausing states rather than escape from promoter-proximal pausing (Aoi et al., 2020). Upon NELF loss, RNA Pol II travels from a first pause region to a second pause region corresponding to the +1 nucleosome dyad (Aoi et al., 2020). We reasoned that the SPT5 depletion system, coupled with the VCP inhibition described earlier, may allow us to precisely investigate the SPT5 function in promoter-proximal pause/release. We performed precision run-on sequencing (PRO-seq) with spike-in normalization (Kwak et al., 2013) to map the RNA Pol II positions at base-pair resolution. Strikingly, RNA Pol II was paused at the second pause regions when depleting SPT5 and inhibiting VCP (Figures 5F–5H), which resembles NELF depletion. We then performed ChIP-seq for the NELF-A subunit and observed a strong reduction of the NELF-A signal when depleting SPT5 and inhibiting VCP (Figure 5I). In this condition, the residual NELF-A signal at promoters was detected (Figure 5I), suggesting a weak NELF-RNA Pol II interaction in the absence of SPT5. Collectively, these results indicate that SPT5 regulates promoter-proximal pausing at the first pause region, likely through its interaction with NELF, and that the transition to the second pause region upon SPT5 depletion is through NELF dissociating from RNA Pol II.

我们最近证明了 NELF 通过调节暂停状态的转变而非从启动子附近暂停中逃逸来发挥体内功能（Aoi 等，2020）。在 NELF 缺失时，RNA Pol II 从第一个暂停区域移动到对应于+1 核小体对称轴（dyad）的第二个暂停区域（Aoi 等，2020）。我们推测，结合前述的 VCP 抑制，SPT5 耗竭系统可能使我们能够精确研究 SPT5 在启动子附近暂停及暂停释放中的功能。我们进行了精确核转录延伸测序（PRO-seq）并使用外源 spike-in 进行归一化（Kwak 等，2013），以在碱基对分辨率下绘制 RNA Pol II 的位置。令人惊讶的是，在耗竭 SPT5 并抑制 VCP 时，RNA Pol II 在第二个暂停区域暂停（图 5F–5H），这类似于 NELF 缺失的情况。随后我们对 NELF-A 亚基进行了 ChIP-seq 分析，并观察到在耗竭 SPT5 并抑制 VCP 时，NELF-A 信号显著减少（图 5I）。在此条件下，在启动子处检测到了残留的 NELF-A 信号（图 5I），表明在没有 SPT5 的情况下，NELF 与 RNA Pol II 的相互作用较弱。综合这些结果表明，SPT5 通过与 NELF 的相互作用调控第一个暂停区域的启动子附近暂停，并且在 SPT5 耗竭时向第二个暂停区域的转变是通过 NELF 从 RNA Pol II 上解离实现的。

To further understand the mechanism of RPB1 degradation during early elongation, we interrogated the requirement for CDK9/P-TEFb, which positively regulates the release from promoter-proximal pausing at most RNA Pol II-transcribed genes (Chen et al., 2018). We treated SPT5-AID cells with the CDK9 inhibitor NVP-2, followed by auxin, and then performed ChIP-seq for RNA Pol II. Surprisingly, CDK9 inhibition stabilized a substantial amount of RNA Pol II in the absence of SPT5 (Figure 6A). RNA Pol II signals were mostly accumulated at the 5′ ends of gene bodies (the promoter-proximal regions) (Figures 6A–6C). To confirm this result, we used THAL-SNS-032, a degrader of CDK9 (Olson et al., 2018). The pharmacological degradation of CDK9 also stabilized RNA Pol II levels (Figures 6A–6C and S6A). Thus, these results suggest that CDK9/P-TEFb function is required for RPB1 degradation at the promoter-proximal regions in the absence of SPT5. RPB1 was not stable when SPT5 was first depleted followed by CDK9 inhibition (Figure S6B). This suggests that CDK9 is required for RPB1 degradation when SPT5 depletion triggers it.

为了进一步理解早期延伸过程中 RPB1 降解的机制，我们探究了 CDK9/P-TEFb 的必要性，因为 CDK9/P-TEFb 正调控大多数 RNA Pol II 转录基因的启动子附近暂停释放（Chen 等，2018）。我们用 CDK9 抑制剂 NVP-2 处理 SPT5-AID 细胞，随后加入生长素，然后对 RNA Pol II 进行染色质免疫沉淀测序。令人惊讶的是，在没有 SPT5 的情况下，CDK9 抑制稳定了大量 RNA Pol II（图 6A）。RNA Pol II 信号主要积累在基因体的 5′ 端（启动子附近区域）（图 6A–6C）。为了确认这一结果，我们使用了 CDK9 的降解剂 THAL-SNS-032（Olson 等，2018）。药物诱导的 CDK9 降解同样稳定了 RNA Pol II 水平（图 6A–6C 和 S6A）。因此，这些结果表明，在没有 SPT5 的情况下，CDK9/P-TEFb 的功能对于启动子附近区域 RPB1 的降解是必需的。当首先耗竭 SPT5 再抑制 CDK9 时，RPB1 并不稳定（图 S6B）。这表明在 SPT5 耗竭触发 RPB1 降解时，CDK9 是必需的。

P-TEFb forms multiple distinct catalytically active complexes with specific functions within each complex (Zheng et al., 2021). For example, SEC-P-TEFb is required for rapid transcriptional induction as a result of heat shock (Lin et al., 2010; Zheng et al., 2021), whereas BRD4-P-TEFb is required for general transcription (Jang et al., 2005; Yang et al., 2005; Zheng et al., 2021). Therefore, we examined the dependence of P-TEFb on SEC or BRD4 in RNA Pol II degradation when depleting SPT5. Unexpectedly, the pharmacological inhibition of SEC-P-TEFb interaction (Liang et al., 2018) or degradation of BRD4 (Winter et al., 2017) did not stabilize RPB1 upon SPT5 loss (Figure 6D), unlike the use of general P-TEFb inhibitors (Figures 6A and 6B). This unexpected finding suggests that a novel form of the P-TEFb complex activated by an unknown factor other than SEC or BRD4 regulates RPB1 degradation in the absence of SPT5.

P-TEFb 形成多个具有特定功能的催化活性复合物（Zheng 等，2021）。例如，SEC-P-TEFb 对热休克引起的快速转录诱导是必需的（Lin 等，2010；Zheng 等，2021），而 BRD4-P-TEFb 对一般转录是必需的（Jang 等，2005；Yang 等，2005；Zheng 等，2021）。因此，我们检查了在耗竭 SPT5 时 P-TEFb 对 SEC 或 BRD4 的依赖性。出乎意料的是，对 SEC-P-TEFb 相互作用的药理学抑制（Liang 等，2018）或 BRD4 的降解（Winter 等，2017）在 SPT5 缺失时并未稳定 RPB1（图 6D），这与使用一般 P-TEFb 抑制剂的情况不同（图 6A 和 6B）。这一意外发现表明，在没有 SPT5 的情况下，由未知因子而非 SEC 或 BRD4 激活的一种新型 P-TEFb 复合物调节 RPB1 的降解。

This study provides detailed mechanistic and biochemical insight into in vivo functions of SPT5. Stabilization of promoter-proximal RNA Pol II by SPT5 is a crucial step to release RNA Pol II into gene bodies (Figure 7A). This regulatory step connects the proper transition from transcriptional initiation to productive elongation to safeguard accurate gene expression. SPT5 is also required for promoter-proximal pausing through NELF recruitment and proper elongation velocity. It is important to determine which comes first in the absence of SPT5: destabilization of RNA Pol II, the pausing defect, or slower velocity. An intriguing possibility is that SPT5 loss leads to unproductive elongation on nucleosomes, which may trigger RPB1 destabilization. Indeed, upon DNA damage, RNA Pol II stalls at transcription-blocking lesions, and this triggers RPB1 destabilization (Gregersen and Svejstrup, 2018). There may be a common mechanism to recognize elongation-incompetent RNA Pol II on chromatin, although distinct E3 ligases are used for RPB1 degradation in each case. Interestingly, NELF depletion does not lead to RPB1 degradation (Figure 1C) while RNA Pol II is incapable of traversing the +1 nucleosome (Aoi et al., 2020). In addition, PAF1 depletion does not lead to RPB1 degradation (Figure 1D) while reducing elongation velocity in long genes (Chen et al., 2017; Hou et al., 2019). Together, these findings suggest that the pausing defect or slower velocity may not be sufficient to lead to RPB1 degradation. This raises another possibility: that SPT5 may regulate RPB1 stability and proper elongation (pausing and velocity) independently. Future studies are needed to dissect these possibilities and elucidate the order of events triggered by SPT5 depletion.

本研究从机制和生化层面详细揭示了 SPT5 的体内功能。SPT5 稳定启动子附近的 RNA Pol II，是 RNA Pol II 释放并进入基因体的关键步骤（图 7A）。这一调控步骤衔接了从转录起始到有效延伸的正常转换，从而保障准确的基因表达。SPT5 还通过招募 NELF 参与启动子附近暂停，并维持正常的延伸速度。在缺乏 SPT5 时，RNA Pol II 失稳、暂停缺陷和延伸减慢，究竟哪一事件最先发生，仍有待确定。一种值得关注的可能性是，SPT5 缺失使 RNA Pol II 无法在核小体上有效延伸，进而触发 RPB1 失稳。事实上，DNA 损伤发生后，RNA Pol II 会停滞于阻碍转录的损伤位点，并由此触发 RPB1 失稳（Gregersen 和 Svejstrup，2018）。细胞可能存在识别染色质上不具备延伸能力的 RNA Pol II 的共同机制，只是在不同情况下使用不同的 E3 连接酶降解 RPB1。值得注意的是，耗竭 NELF 后，RNA Pol II 虽无法穿过 +1 核小体（Aoi 等，2020），却不会发生 RPB1 降解（图 1C）。同样，耗竭 PAF1 虽降低了长基因上的延伸速度（Chen 等，2017；Hou 等，2019），也不会导致 RPB1 降解（图 1D）。这些发现提示，暂停缺陷或延伸减慢本身可能不足以引发 RPB1 降解。这又提出了另一种可能性：SPT5 可能分别调控 RPB1 稳定性和正常延伸（暂停及速度）。未来仍需研究来区分这些可能性，并阐明 SPT5 耗竭所触发事件的先后顺序。

SPT5 depletion leads to destabilization of promoter-proximal RNA Pol II, but NELF depletion does not, and both depletions result in a pausing shift to the + 1 nucleosome (Aoi et al., 2020). One of the major differences is that SPT5 depletion leads to NELF loss, but NELF depletion does not lead to SPT5 loss (Aoi et al., 2020). This raises the possibility that SPT5 protects promoter-proximal RNA Pol II from RPB1 degradation in the absence of NELF (Figure 7B). This model is consistent with the previous finding that NELF-depleted RNA Pol II undergoes premature termination at promoter-proximal regions (Aoi et al., 2020), rather than destabilization of RNA Pol II. It is likely that RNA Pol II is capable of termination as long as SPT5 interacts with it.

SPT5 的耗竭导致启动子附近 RNA Pol II 不稳定，但 NELF 的耗竭则不会，且两种耗竭均导致暂停位点向 +1 核小体转移（Aoi 等，2020）。其中一个主要区别是，SPT5 的耗竭导致 NELF 丢失，而 NELF 的耗竭并不会导致 SPT5 丢失（Aoi 等，2020）。这提出了一个可能性，即在缺乏 NELF 的情况下，SPT5 保护启动子附近的 RNA Pol II 免受 RPB1 降解（图 7B）。这一模型与先前发现一致，即 NELF 耗竭的 RNA Pol II 在启动子附近区域发生过早终止（Aoi 等，2020），而非 RNA Pol II 不稳定。RNA Pol II 似乎只要与 SPT5 相互作用，就能够进行终止。

We identify the ubiquitin ligase CUL3 as a novel proteolysis machinery targeting RPB1 in the absence of SPT5 (Figures 2, 3, and 7A). We also show that neither NEDD4 nor CUL5 is required for this RPB1 degradation (Figure 3), whereas NEDD4 or CUL5 is required for damage-induced RPB1 degradation (Anindya et al., 2007; Yasukawa et al., 2008). These results reveal that the CUL3-RPB1 degradation pathway in the absence of SPT5 is distinct from the damage-induced RPB1 degradation pathway. Intriguingly, the VCP unfoldase is required for both degradation pathways (Figure 4; Gregersen and Svejstrup, 2018; He et al., 2017). We speculate that cells may use different ligases for RPB1 ubiquitylation in a context-dependent manner, whereas removal of ubiquitylated RPB1 from chromatin generally depends on the unfolding function of VCP.

我们鉴定出泛素连接酶 CUL3 是在缺乏 SPT5 时靶向 RPB1 的一种新的蛋白降解机制的组成部分（图 2、3 和 7A）。我们还发现，该 RPB1 降解过程既不需要 NEDD4，也不需要 CUL5（图 3）；而损伤诱导的 RPB1 降解则需要 NEDD4 或 CUL5（Anindya 等，2007；Yasukawa 等，2008）。这些结果表明，SPT5 缺失时的 CUL3-RPB1 降解途径有别于损伤诱导的 RPB1 降解途径。值得关注的是，这两种降解途径都需要解折叠酶 VCP（图 4；Gregersen 和 Svejstrup，2018；He 等，2017）。我们推测，细胞可能根据具体情境使用不同的连接酶使 RPB1 泛素化，而从染色质上移除泛素化 RPB1 则通常依赖 VCP 的解折叠功能。

How the degradation machinery recognizes RPB1 upon SPT5 loss remains unknown. When depleting SPT5 and inhibiting VCP, RNA Pol II was accumulated at the 5′ end of genes, but RNA Pol II distribution at the rest of the gene bodies was surprisingly normal (Figure 5). Our data also demonstrate that RNA Pol II can persist in the late elongation stage in the absence of SPT5 (Figure 5). These features may distinguish the SPT5-RPB1 degradation pathway from previously reported RPB1 degradation pathways, in which RNA Pol II is irreversibly stalled by DNA lesions, RNA Pol II inhibitors, or the inability to resolve backtracked RNA Pol II (Gregersen and Svejstrup, 2018). A recent study predicted that SPT5 may block the recruitment of the yeast Rad26 to lesion-stalled RNA Pol II upon DNA damage (Xu et al., 2017). SPT5 loss may stimulate recruitment of a degradation apparatus, including Rad26, to RNA Pol II. One could speculate that SPT5 acts as a physiological switch of RPB1 degradation.

在失去 SPT5 后，降解机制如何识别 RPB1 仍不清楚。当耗竭 SPT5 并抑制 VCP 时，RNA Pol II 在基因的 5′ 端积累，但 RNA Pol II 在其余基因体上的分布却出人意料地正常（图 5）。我们的数据还表明，在没有 SPT5 的情况下，RNA Pol II 可以在延伸的后期阶段持续存在（图 5）。这些特征可能将 SPT5-RPB1 降解通路与之前报道的 RPB1 降解通路区分开来，后者中 RNA Pol II 被 DNA 损伤、RNA Pol II 抑制剂或无法解决回溯的 RNA Pol II 所不可逆地停滞（Gregersen 和 Svejstrup，2018）。最近的一项研究预测，SPT5 可能阻止酵母 Rad26 在 DNA 损伤后与停滞的 RNA Pol II 结合（Xu 等，2017）。失去 SPT5 可能刺激一个降解装置的招募，包括 Rad26，到 RNA Pol II 上。据此可以推测，SPT5 可能充当 RPB1 降解的生理性开关。

We show that an initiation defect stimulates an alternative RPB1 degradation pathway in a VCP-independent manner (Figure 4). Although it is unknown how RPB1 is degraded without VCP’s function, our data underline multiple degradation pathways for RPB1. We assume that upon inhibition of initiation, RPB1 predominantly exists as a free form in the nucleoplasm, because chromatin-bound RNA Pol II undergoes termination. This free RPB1 may be rapidly degraded without the unfolding function of VCP. The maintenance of the free RNA Pol II levels is associated with human diseases such as Cockayne syndrome (Tufegdzic Vidakovic et al., 2020), highlighting a physiological significance of VCP-independent RNA Pol II stability.

我们表明，起始缺陷会刺激一种不依赖于 VCP 的替代 RPB1 降解通路（图 4）。尽管目前尚不清楚在没有 VCP 功能的情况下 RPB1 是如何被降解的，但我们的数据强调了 RPB1 存在多种降解通路。我们假设，在抑制起始后，RPB1 主要以游离形式存在于核质中，因为染色质结合的 RNA Pol II 经历终止过程。这种游离的 RPB1 可能在没有 VCP 解折叠功能的情况下迅速被降解。维持游离 RNA Pol II 水平与人类疾病如 Cockayne 综合征有关（Tufegdžić Vidaković 等，2020），突显了不依赖于 VCP 的 RNA Pol II 稳定性的生理意义。

Our data suggest that a CDK9 complex, perhaps with an unknown activator other than SEC or BRD4, is required for RPB1 degradation upon SPT5 loss (Figure 6). Although CDK9 is known to be activated by SEC and BRD4 and inactivated by the HEXIM-7SK small nuclear ribonucleoprotein particle complex (Chen et al., 2018), how CDK9 regulates RNA Pol II transcription without SEC or BRD4 remains unknown. Future in vivo studies will identify an active form of the CDK9 complex, as well as its target phosphorylation sites that are required for RPB1 degradation in the absence of SPT5.

我们的数据表明，在 SPT5 缺失时，需要一个 CDK9 复合物，可能还有未知的激活因子而非 SEC 或 BRD4，来促进 RPB1 的降解（图 6）。尽管已知 CDK9 由 SEC 和 BRD4 激活，并被 HEXIM-7SK 小核核糖核蛋白颗粒复合物抑制（Chen 等，2018），但 CDK9 如何在没有 SEC 或 BRD4 的情况下调节 RNA Pol II 转录仍不清楚。未来的体内研究将鉴定出 CDK9 复合物的活性形式及其所需的靶标磷酸化位点，这些对于在没有 SPT5 的情况下进行 RPB1 降解是必需的。

We demonstrate that the SPT5-VCP pathway for RPB1 stability is functionally conserved in yeast Saccharomyces cerevisiae and human cells (Figures 4 and S4). In yeast Schizosaccharomyces pombe, acute depletion of SPT5 has been shown to change the distribution of RNA Pol II, resulting in an accumulation at the 5′ end of genes and decreased RNA Pol II levels downstream (Shetty et al., 2017). Total RPB1 levels appear to remain relatively unchanged after SPT5 depletion in S. pombe (Shetty et al., 2017). Because S. pombe and S. cerevisiae show distinct regulations for RNA Pol II transcription (Booth et al., 2016), it is possible that depletion of S. pombe SPT5 only affects RNA Pol II distribution and does not trigger RPB1 degradation. However, we cannot rule out the contribution of experimental differences in the two studies. Future studies using different organisms with acute depletion methods will be needed to explore the conservation of RPB1 degradation in the absence of SPT5.

我们证明了 SPT5-VCP 通路对于 RPB1 稳定性在酵母 Saccharomyces cerevisiae 和人类细胞中具有功能保守性（图 4 和图 S4）。在裂殖酵母 Schizosaccharomyces pombe 中，急性耗竭 SPT5 已被证明会改变 RNA Pol II 的分布，导致其在基因 5′ 端积累，并且下游 RNA Pol II 水平下降（Shetty 等，2017 年）。在 S. pombe 中 SPT5 耗竭后总 RPB1 水平似乎保持相对不变（Shetty 等，2017 年）。由于 S. pombe 和 S. cerevisiae 对 RNA Pol II 转录表现出不同的调控机制（Booth 等，2016 年），因此可能 S. pombe 中 SPT5 的耗竭仅影响 RNA Pol II 的分布而不触发 RPB1 降解。然而，我们不能排除两项研究之间实验差异的贡献。未来需要使用不同生物体并采用急性耗竭方法的研究来探索在缺乏 SPT5 的情况下 RPB1 降解的保守性。

Establishment of promoter-proximal pausing is a metazoan-specific function of SPT5. In S. cerevisiae, RNA Pol II appears not to undergo a promoter-proximal pausing step (Steinmetz et al., 2006), and its genome lacks the pausing factor NELF (Narita et al., 2003). Together with these findings, our work suggests that the stabilization of RNA Pol II is one of SPT5’s primary functions in eukaryotes, whereas metazoans have acquired promoter-proximal pausing as a secondary regulatory function.

启动子附近暂停的建立是 SPT5 在后生动物中的特有功能。在 S. cerevisiae 中，RNA Pol II 似乎不会经历启动子附近的暂停步骤（Steinmetz 等，2006 年），并且其基因组缺乏暂停因子 NELF（Narita 等，2003 年）。结合这些发现，我们的工作表明 RNA Pol II 的稳定化是 SPT5 在真核生物中的主要功能之一，而后生动物则获得了启动子附近暂停作为次要调控功能。

Limitations of the study ｜研究的局限性#

We show evidence for CUL3-RPB1 interaction by proteomics and ChIP-seq experiments, but our data cannot exclude the possibility that this interaction is indirect. To test whether the CUL3-RPB1 interaction is direct, it is crucial to identify a substrate recognition subunit of the CUL3-RING complex among nearly 200 candidate subunits in the human genome (Dubiel et al., 2018). It is also possible that CUL3 recognizes another RNA Pol II subunit or RNA Pol II-associated proteins to target RPB1 degradation in the absence of SPT5. The AID system allows gene-specific depletion, unlike shRNA knockdown, which has off-target effects. We show that in parental cells (no AID tag), auxin treatment does not lead to RPB1 degradation. This control is sufficient to claim that SPT5-AID depletion leads to RPB1 degradation. It would be interesting to test whether a disruption of SPT5-RPB1 interaction leads to RPB1 degradation by rescue experiments.

我们通过蛋白质组学和染色质免疫沉淀测序实验展示了 CUL3 与 RPB1 的相互作用，但我们的数据无法排除这种相互作用是间接的可能性。为了测试 CUL3-RPB1 相互作用是否为直接相互作用，关键在于在人类基因组中近 200 个候选亚基中鉴定出 CUL3-RING 复合物的底物识别亚基（Dubiel 等，2018 年）。也有可能 CUL3 识别另一种 RNA Pol II 亚基或 RNA Pol II 相关蛋白以在缺乏 SPT5 的情况下靶向 RPB1 降解。AID 系统允许基因特异性耗竭，而不同于 shRNA 敲低可能具有脱靶效应。我们显示，在亲本细胞（无 AID 标签）中，生长素处理不会导致 RPB1 降解。这一对照足以证明 SPT5-AID 耗竭导致 RPB1 降解。通过拯救实验测试 SPT5-RPB1 相互作用的破坏是否会导致 RPB1 降解会很有趣。
